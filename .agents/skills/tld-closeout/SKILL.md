## Preconditions
Locate explicit approval of a specific version and exact candidate bytes. Verify current approved archive and all required candidate evidence. No inferred approval from scores, liking a passage, silence or request for a draft. If accepted version unavailable, report blocker and do not invent approval or content.
## Transaction
Lock exact approved text and SHA-256. Preserve unchanged prior units and their hashes, including supplements; never replace a prior accepted version silently. Authorized revisions require new version, dependency audit and retained historical retirement.
Execute NDM 1.0/2.0 on accepted bytes: lexical/paragraph/syntax metrics with declared method, abstract scene functions and usage, distinctive expressions retired permanently, semantic signatures and local motif exceptions. Link existing abstract mechanisms rather than inflate counts; new distinct candidates only when supported. Merge candidate continuity delta transactionally into approved registry; preserve historical states, timestamps, knowledge acquisition and unknowns. Validate source locators, chronology, balances, ID links and status.
Build next private archive version, manifest, approved exact text, retirement/usage/exception ledgers, continuity registry, chapter_distillation, reports and appropriate validators. Execute archive integrity, hash and supplement-aware regression, future guard and continuity validation. Verify actual saved private archive and readback; a temporary ZIP alone is not persistent registration. Preserve the previous archive. If any check fails, do not promote status; repair or report.
Only after private verification, update public-safe production/originality status, aggregate mechanisms and applicable protocol references through tld-repository. No private prose, distinctive language, spoilers or detailed continuity records in public Git. Recheck public ref and paths; G6 closes only with verified private archive and explicit approval.
## Output
Exact revision/hash, private archive locator, actual checks and scoped limitations, public commit SHA, current production state. No automatic next chapter or publication; author remains the literary approver.
