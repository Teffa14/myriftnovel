## Draft from the verified brief
Require tld-chapter-planning and applicable tld-competitive-evidence. Write original idiomatic American English, close third-person past, one focal character per scene. Present-tense portraits only within the authorized interlude framing. Concrete early anchor, natural movement, distinct voice and independent motivation. Normally 1,700–2,200 words, extend if earned; never pad or force ratios.
Use abstract narrative functions, not source language or recycled scenes. Selective sensory detail, varied sentence/paragraph cadence, clear causal transitions, precise verbs, readable technical context. Prefer observable behavior and subtext over explanatory interior monologue, generic motivation or praise. Avoid stock contrasts, repetitive sentence openings and gestures, needless synonyms, translation artifacts, overfragmentation and formulaic cliffhangers. Do not introduce deliberate mistakes to evade detectors or claim human authorship.
For a technical exchange, show meaningful choice, cost, response, adaptation and consequence. The novice needs stakes and why the choice matters; a veteran needs mechanically correct specificity. Do not turn narration into a rules manual or omit the decisive exchange from a match-focused chapter. Preserve POV knowledge; no hidden-card certainty.
For shops, tournaments and chats consult community resources. Populate people with independent errands, personal interests, ordinary interruptions, distinct opinions and occasional nonsense; not everyone competes or cares about Nico. Never reuse actual Reddit wording or identities. For recurring extras, register proposed details in private continuity. For interludes and character appearance, preserve established identities, ordinary tastes and adult attractiveness without spoilers, automatic romance or unapproved facts. Promotional art is not automatic canon.
Write actual private draft, not a prompt to another author. Use accessible writing/editorial skills as lenses where relevant; do not claim unavailable plugins or independent reviewers ran. Style Bible governs; unrelated personal writing and distinctive source styles are not generation templates.
