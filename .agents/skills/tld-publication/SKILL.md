## Reality-based commercial assessment
Read current production status; distinguish approved, draft, published and platform-eligible words. Verify current platform policies, application requirements, program eligibility, rights, exclusivity, AI disclosure and compensation directly when material. Dated guide claims are historical evidence; no contract guarantee or income estimate without assumptions. Do not substitute archive word count for published platform eligibility. Resolve real-game IP/cover rights and licensing; no implied Riot endorsement, no automatic fan-content monetization.
Retain exact author-selected title and approved branding unless separately authorized. Prepare reader-facing concise promise separate from private editor-facing causal outline, named relevant character profiles, evidence-based differentiation and truthful disclosure. Compare dated relevant debut works and ranking windows; do not infer readership from views/likes or force romance/cheat tropes into realistic sports fiction.
Use consent-based audience validation, actual cohorts and retention data, not invented metrics. Estimate revenue scenarios with explicit conditional assumptions, fees/program terms and zero-income baseline; review sustainable cadence and reserve versus proven production capacity. Separate literary acceptance from commercial G5. 
## Approval boundary
No submitting applications, signing, public release, uploading cover/manuscript, or changing rights/contract settings without explicit authorization and required gate checks. Keep unpublished outline and manuscript private. Provide a readiness report, concrete blockers, next achievable milestones and source dates.
