# Social realism and independent background characters
Binding author direction: background people must feel like players one could encounter repeatedly in a real card shop, including mundane chatter, nonsense, petty disagreements, humor, small kindnesses and interests unrelated to the protagonist. They are people, not tutorial prompts, cheering crowds, plot vending machines or a single sarcastic voice.
 
## Scene construction
Select the location/time/occasion and who would realistically be there for their own reason. In a shop, people are registering, buying sleeves, hunting singles, comparing pulls, checking delivery, arranging rides, eating, waiting for pairings, trading, playing another game, discussing work, or trying to finish before closing. Not every conversation is about Riftbound; some characters are collectors, companions or staff. Model two independent motives before choosing any exchange. Let conversations start before Nico arrives, end without him, be interrupted by an ordinary obligation, or have no later plot payoff. Keep focalization: he can overhear only what is audible, and cannot know a stranger's thoughts. Do not gratuitously turn every background line into an important clue.
 
Use a varied social ecology. A person can be helpful and ruthless in a match; annoyed about bans and generous with cards; a collector who never plays; a competitive regular who goes home because of work. Distinguish expertise, confidence, personality, fatigue, circumstances and relationships. Do not infer personal traits from identity or assign a real Reddit user's speech or conduct to a character. Use respectful portrayals and situational disagreement rather than caricature. Mix humor with genuinely unfunny speech, half sentences, unfinished talk, practical questions and unremarkable answers. Use casual American English, familiar terms such as locals, NN, LGS, prizing, jank, brew, staples, singles, pulls, ON, sig, foil, cut, scoop, topdeck, brick, mirror, tech, sideboard, and matchup where context makes sense. This glossary is ordinary TCG language, not a frequency claim; use it in context, not as a slang quota. Online abbreviations need not appear in spoken conversation. Avoid excessive slang, forced profanity or uniform banter.
 
## Opinions vs facts
A speaker may express a wrong prediction, exaggerated complaint, unsourced rumor, bad heuristic or subjective price judgment. Attribute it to the speaker, avoid authorial endorsement, distinguish actual known facts and leave uncertainty where appropriate. If a mechanical claim determines an outcome, verify exact dated rules/card text. Do not give fictional accusations real targets or reproduce actual private conflicts. No Reddit comment is official event policy. Never use future information in a past scene. The snapshot is available as of Nov 25 2026; older format-specific claims require date checks, even for background dialogue.
 
## Persistent extras
When introducing a recurring fictional background person, privately allocate a stable ID, venue, independent goal, time constraints, relation to other people, register, current deck/collection if established, last appearance, unresolved ordinary concern and what they know. Track what changes off page plausibly: they miss an event, sell a card, get a new shift, lose interest, try another deck, or meet another friend. Don't invent precise state retroactively. Some extras remain unnamed and never return; no quota of names or chatter. Keep minor character continuity separate from main cast canon, promote only when earned. A returning extra need not explain their entire history or address the protagonist. Never reuse the same verbal gimmick automatically. 
## Production checks
Read relevant community source observations and abstract profiles before writing; select by scene purpose, not quota. Write fresh lines. The 36 exercises and 16 situations are noncanon research examples, not a dialogue copy bank. Check any draft against approved private NDM 2.0 archive and avoid exact or semantic reuse of examples. In editorial pass ask whether background people would plausibly speak if Nico were absent, whether at least some activity has an independent purpose, whether emotional registers differ, whether factual claims are correctly attributed, and whether scene pace is still appropriate. No compulsory background insertions in private household scenes or high-focus duels. Record actual used patterns and new recurring-character state after approval.
