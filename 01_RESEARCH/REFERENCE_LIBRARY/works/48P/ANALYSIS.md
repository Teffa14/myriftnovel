# 48P — all 48 source units, transformative editorial mapping
Source: supplied Spanish PDF, physical pages 2–30. The cover says this is a condensed guide; text contains imperative maxims, short explanations, some quotations and historical anecdotes, not narrative scenes. Preserve author/compendium attribution uncertainty; the quoted authors and historical claims have not been independently verified. Treat the laws as claims about power, not proven causal laws. The source is asymmetrical and frequently endorses exploitation; the novel can depict the resulting choices, resistance, accountability and costs without adopting the prescriptions. Each row names a broad source theme and a distinct original scene possibility. No source passage is reproduced. A numbered unit's transition is represented by a possible fictional decision and its constraint, not a source plot. All applications are editorial hypotheses and noncanonical.

## Tensions to retain
Visibility and selective absence (6/16) depend on an established audience and current obligations. Independence and mutual reliance (11/18/20/43) conflict when a person needs help but also values freedom. Recognition, credit and the appearance of effortless competence (5/7/26/30/46) can produce pressure to conceal work or mistakes; credible correction is a competing response. Decisiveness, preparation, patience and stopping (28/29/35/47) create meaningful decisions under uncertain time and budget constraints. Predictability and adaptation (17/38/45/48) require different responses in a match, a team, and an organization. Do not resolve these conflicts into a single infallible doctrine.

## Transfer boundaries
For fictional social conflicts, separate what a character wants, what they know, what they can actually offer, what another person can refuse, and what changes afterward. Preserve consequences over time. For competitive scenes, FRY and the scoped game rules govern legal interaction. No cheating, intimidation, privacy intrusion, financial coercion, social sabotage, unsupported allegations against real people, fabricated win probabilities or inferred hidden information. Historical material is context only; no claim of factual verification. No new canon.
