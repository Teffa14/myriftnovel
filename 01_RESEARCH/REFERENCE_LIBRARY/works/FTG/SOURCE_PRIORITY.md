## Evidence and interpretation
The guide's 1–10 complexity, meta outlook and fit are editorial ratings, not measured probabilities. The 55 new-rules, 77 pre-ban and 26 archive list occurrences belong to different evidence windows. The latter two categories must not be used as post-ban legal proof. Per-card counts measure inclusion across lists, not copies, games or wins. Chosen-champion and main-deck counts are distinct; sideboard is not included. Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman and Wuju Master are separate Legend contexts. Forecasts (40 profiles) are hypotheses, not results. Date every use; never apply these assessments automatically to later sets.
