## Three complementary research lenses
FRY: assess the contest, available legal options, initiative, and feedback. 48P: examine incentives, institutional authority, reputation and obligations. MED: examine one's judgments and conduct, even when outcomes or the opinions of others cannot be controlled. These are analytical lenses, not a synthetic universal doctrine. A strategically effective action may be ethically unacceptable, an ethical action may cost an opportunity, and a good decision may still lead to defeat. The narrative should retain these distinctions and give characters genuine disagreement.

## Boundaries
Self-government is not suppression, denial of distress, medical treatment, or a requirement to endure harassment. Acceptance of a fact does not imply approving its cause or abandoning justified action. Stoic claims about external goods, death, providence and cosmic order are historically situated; do not transfer them as verified science or moral absolutes. Never erase losses, financial constraints, rule violations or reporting responsibilities. No player is required to adopt the author's metaphysical beliefs. Use fictional decisions, not lectures, and preserve existing character agency and goals.
