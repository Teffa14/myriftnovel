## Three original conceptual exercises
A playtester finds an impressive payoff stranded too often. The group compares a cheaper option under equivalent initial conditions, preserving failed trials. No optimality claim from one success.
A player adds a threat whose value requires remaining in play. Another recognizes that it creates a productive target for an otherwise weak answer. They compare configurations under the same assumptions, not a predetermined victory.
Two teammates propose different answers to a recurring loss. One checks access probability, the other legal timing. Drawing an answer and being able to use it are different constraints.
No named effects, actual match outcomes or canon assertions.
