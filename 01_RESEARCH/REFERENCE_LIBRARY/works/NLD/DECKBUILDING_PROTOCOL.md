# NLD — deck design and matchup protocol
Use NLM for in-game decisions, NLD for construction, preparation and adaptation. Official dated Riftbound rules/card evidence, canon and G3-scene remain authoritative; this is not a game engine.

Privately register format, date, region, legal deck and rune constraints, relevant verified legend/battlefield/chosen-card effects, objective and scoring route. Assign cards primary and secondary roles, duplicates, timing, costs, resource types, restrictions, prerequisites, fallback, ownership and budget. Verify exact composition as required by scene scope. Never import MTG land, life, color, starting-hand, deck-size, Commander or direct-damage rules.

## Workflow
1. Freeze baseline/candidate; state change, reason and falsifiable hypothesis.
2. Build dated gauntlet of representative legal lists; distinguish pilots, matchups, play/draw, seeds and post-board conditions.
3. Measure opening access, joint resource constraints, duplicate cost, time to viable scoring, answer coverage and disruption exposure. Define actual population/sampling procedure, including guaranteed/chosen cards and mulligans; never treat access probability as win probability.
4. Compare evidence with sample size, uncertainty, alternative explanations and costs. Retain failures; no optimality claim from small samples.
5. Record legal post-board swap counts and resulting plan; preserve composition and resource support.
6. Reevaluate after releases, bans, rules changes, matchup evidence and budget. Preserve historical versions.
