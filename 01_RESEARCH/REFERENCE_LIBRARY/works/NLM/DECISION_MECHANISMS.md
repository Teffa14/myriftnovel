# NLM — 45 competitive decision mechanisms
Candidates distilled from the supplied strategy book for editorial production. Related IDs identify overlaps; these are not 45 independently certified novel theories. All examples are original conceptual applications, not established canon or mechanically verified matches. Use in combination with SCENE_INTEGRITY.md; all actual card text, rules, state, priority and scoring must be sourced. The sections on persuasion are constrained to legal observation, epistemic uncertainty, respectful communication and self-regulation. Do not use them as a manipulation manual.

## NLM-M01 — Self-inventory
Source: pp. 6–13, 20–23, 35–41, 162–177. Related: FRY-S01; MED-M04.
Decision structure: Specific observable error → baseline → one target → later comparison.
Original application: A player documents a timing mistake rather than calling himself bad.
Rejection / acceptance: Track variance and controllable choices; avoid public disclosure mandates or perfectionism.
Status: extracted; no independent reader or Riftbound game test.

## NLM-M02 — Practice-feedback loop
Source: pp. 10–13, 79–83, 120–125, 398–399. Related: FRY-S09; MED-M04.

Original application: A team changes one variable after recording a failed trial.
Rejection / acceptance: Record sample and alternative explanations; no causal claim from one result.
Status: extracted; no independent reader or Riftbound game test.
