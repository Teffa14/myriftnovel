# NLM — operational transfer for Riftbound and narrative
Research priority: use as a central TCG strategy lens; official dated Riftbound rules/card evidence remain authoritative. This adds no new game mechanic. The word "stage" is an analytical model, not a formal game phase.

## Decision record
For every material competitive choice, privately register: scene ID, ruleset/format/date, current public state and POV-known private information, unknown variables, objective, candidate mechanisms, each legal option considered, expected consequences and uncertainty, actual action, opponent response, verified resulting state, narrative consequence. Add time-to-close estimate only as a conditional scenario, with explicit assumptions. Record separate resource dimensions (cards, board, energy/power/runes as actually defined, legal response windows, scoring progress), not a universal point-value formula. Avoid redundant analysis on routine plays.

## Four-pass selector
1. Top-down: what viable endpoint or payoff matters in this matchup? What is the rival trying to achieve?
2. Bottom-up: what resource, prerequisite, legality issue or knowledge gap currently limits that plan?
3. Backward: what necessary state must precede the objective, and which paths can reach it?
4. Forward: execute a legal trace or scoped calculation; inspect results and update the next decision. Do not turn a fictional target into a fabricated forced result.

## Skill progression
Track error classes privately: legality, overlooked public effect, resource calculation, role misassignment, option omission, unsupported inference, overcommitment, clock management, preparation and review. Evidence of growth requires observable change in repeated decisions under varied conditions. Use baseline and new test; no claimed improvement from a winning streak. Nico's existing over-analysis can make his off-table review stronger than live selection; June's preparation can encounter novel states; Eli's innovation can fail or succeed on test evidence; Marcus's mentorship can teach procedures without match coaching. Existing canon remains authoritative; no hidden personality assignments.

## Transfer rules
MTG land drops, initial hand size, 20 life, card colors, priority/stack, combat, 60/75-card numbers, forty-card Limited and particular historical cards are not Riftbound facts. Runes, battlefield control, movement, scoring, final-point restrictions, legal timing, deck format and mulligan procedure require current official sources. Do not substitute thematic resonance for exact effects. Preserve opponent agency; hidden hand is unknown unless legitimately revealed. Psychological material is limited to permissible observation, humility, communication and self-regulation; no harassment, illegal signals, delay, misrepresentation or mind-reading. Do not assert skill ratings, matchup win percentages or actual tournament outcomes based on the book.

## Illustrative analytical fixtures
The accompanying mathematics is game-agnostic: hypergeometric access probability assumes simple sampling without replacement; expected utility assumes explicit probabilities and utility estimates; dominance requires one option be at least as good in every specified state and strictly better in at least one. Neither calculation proves a real move optimal. Compare outcomes across states, not arbitrary sums of incomparable resources.

The primary narrative application is the difference between competent decisions and successful outcomes. A player can act well and lose; a mistake can be masked by a lucky result. Keep strategic evidence separate from character ethics (MED), social incentives (48P), agency and meaning (MAJ), and broader competitive principles (FRY). Use the relevant lens rather than reciting the corpus.
