## Key transfer
Agency is meaningful access to decisions, not immunity to variance. Model resource curves over several turns; explicitly account for permanent resource expenditure and the opportunity lost when choosing current action. Matchup preparation should remain adaptable to local and regional diversity. A favorable combat margin can create difficult opposing choices without requiring opponent error. When behind, compare risk against the best available conservative line; preserve a fallback when possible. Score planning must use current final-point rules rather than fixed parity dogma. Repeated reinforcement and relocation are hypotheses until exact windows are checked. The source's last three cases are partial recollections, not replay evidence.

## Narrative application
Distinct competitors can choose the same list for different reasons: qualification goals, local familiarity, personal enjoyment, budget or confidence in execution. A player may defend a reasonable risky decision even after a loss, while a teammate identifies a missing alternative.
