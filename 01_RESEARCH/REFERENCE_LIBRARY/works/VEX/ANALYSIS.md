## Decision model
Unlike EKK's narrow scoring burst, this guide prioritizes a repeatable hold engine, resource availability, defensive uncertainty and a late conversion. Both pilots treat the role as conditional: the player who cannot establish a hold may need to take initiative; mobility can redirect pressure, a small loss can preserve a response, and cards in hand may be less valuable than usable runes. FTG FT-L55 rates Vex 9/10 meta outlook, 6/10 complexity, four new-rules lists; these are separate editorial judgments, not validation of any specialist build.

Heron, Kharox and Illaoi create different dependency structures. Heron needs a safe position and a useful subsequent play. The Empower variant trades early payoff and flexible responses for location-sensitive enablers and opposing-trash uncertainty. Illaoi needs generator/payoff balance and distributed defenses. Hybrid and experimental lists exist; no permanent mandatory variant. For scene production choose the build consistent with dated legal card availability and character goals/budget, then compare independently verified candidate lines.

## Evidence boundaries
The authors describe testing but the supplied record images are missing. Their matrix is 58 subjective labels, all mapped to FTG; eight image-code identities resolved by 1,408-row repository catalog screen and exact Legend titles. Riven identified by its explicit image slug. No games/wins or statistical method supplied. The five locked advanced sections (Rune Management, Tips & Tricks, Flex slots and replacements, Our worst nightmares, How to win against US) remain unavailable. Public matchup summary is present; full matchup discussion is not.

Two older lists have 39 main + chosen, 10 sideboard, 3 battlefields and 12 runes; both contain two Hourglass copies, banned in the Nov 23 Standard snapshot. Sneaky Hunters has 40 listed main plus chosen and nine side; ambiguous whether chosen was duplicated or another error. Do not repair by invention. All three inventories retained for count checks only; validate card identities, copies, format, official rules and effective dates before use. Other list images have missing names; never reconstruct unnamed cards from quantities. Five budget configurations are described although introduction says four; four exact historical EUR prices. Heron and chosen-champion prices are source statements (about EUR 175 for Heron acquisition, EUR 16 for Apathetic), quantity/timing and market not independently established. Budget examples mostly dated Nov 13, not reliable post-ban purchases; price not transferred to fictional US acquisition.

Radiance reviews are paired evaluations; identities absent in 20 source rows or inferred only by later context in some others. Keep unknown names null; never claim image inspection. Ten discussed bans agree with repository snapshot, but relative effects remain opinion and historical examples cannot be used post-ban. Source also has inconsistent meta language, ambiguous shorthand, unverified timing and card-effect claims; retain as questions for G3-scene.

## Narrative transfer
Use the contrasting pilot voices to design practice disagreements about slot value, timing, metagame risk and favorite cards. Give each fictional player a private reason to favor one build (time, budget, confidence, local opponents, taste). Show actual tests that change a choice; do not borrow wording, personas, results, or invent a real player biography. A defensive player can be proactive when recovering; an aggressive player can wait. The contrast with EKK creates natural rivalry and tactical diversity, not a prescribed outcome. No canon deck assignment.
