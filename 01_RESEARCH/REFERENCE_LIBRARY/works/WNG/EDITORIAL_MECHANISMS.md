# WNG — editorial and commercial mechanisms
Twenty candidate controls, not independent causal findings or certified narrative techniques. All transfers are original editorial abstractions. Existing G1–G6, NDM 2.0, scene integrity and author approval remain authoritative.

## WNG-M01 — Familiar setting as entry point
Source: S01 Topic Selection; S02 Selecting a Topic.
Transfer: Use concrete ordinary-life constraints as reader access before specialized competition. A work shift, budget or relationship has meaning without TCG knowledge.
Acceptance: Opening test: nonplayer can identify immediate goal, constraint and stakes without glossary.
Boundary: Reject supernatural shortcut or generic humiliation as compulsory.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M02 — Genre promise and compatible trends
Source: S01 Topic Selection; S02 Market Research; S06 ranking preparation.
Transfer: Observe discoverability and common appeals; adopt a function only if consistent with realistic sports fiction.
Acceptance: Record dated comparison title, audience promise, initial conflict, mechanism and applicability; separate observed metrics from inference.
Boundary: No claim that examples from 2023 or 2025 describe current demand.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M03 — Goal hierarchy
Source: S01 Outline objectives; S02 Objectives.
Transfer: Separate overarching desire, arc outcome, chapter objective and scene action. Each short goal materially relates to a longer question or independent subplot.
Acceptance: Trace each goal to an existing canon ID, actor, obstacle and measurable consequence.
Boundary: No mandatory revenge or treasure hunt.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M04 — Credible opposition
Source: S01 Outline objectives; S01 Characterization; S02 Conflicts.
Transfer: Build an opponent with independent goals, evidence of ability and a legitimate constraint on the protagonist. Establish reputation before a meeting only through grounded information.
Acceptance: Rival has own objective, available choices, demonstrated competence and possibility of success.
Boundary: No crowd worship, universal bullying or inevitable protagonist victory.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M05 — Expanding competitive geography
Source: S01 Map design; S02 Map Design.
Transfer: Progress through increasingly demanding venues, relationships and opportunities when prior access, schedule and resources justify expansion.
Acceptance: Track location, travel, calendar, eligibility, obligations and newly available opportunities.
Boundary: No invented tournament calendar; no forced expansion merely for novelty.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M06 — Legible character identity
Source: S01 Characterization; S02 Characterization; S05 Main Characters.
Transfer: Introduce relevant personality, motivation, ordinary role and contradiction through decisions; retain stable traits and earned changes.
Acceptance: Reader identifies character by choices and motive without relying on physical description or archetype label.
Boundary: No appearance-only profiles or forced personality collapse.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M07 — Accumulating conflict
Source: S01 Disagreements; S02 Contradictions and Conflicts.
Transfer: An earlier choice produces an unresolved cost that interacts with another person's objective or limited resources.
Acceptance: Record origin, escalation, plausible alternatives, consequence and continuity link.
Boundary: Reject manufacturing random hostility, jealousy or betrayal without cause.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M08 — Early disturbance and opportunity
Source: S01 Opening; S02 Beginning; S04 opening criteria; S06 chapter 1 example.
Transfer: Establish protagonist, current pressure, consequential choice and a plausible route toward change early. Use actual skill evidence rather than a cheat reveal.
Acceptance: Respect existing pilot's first-150-word opening constraint; assess reader comprehension and anticipation separately.
Boundary: Do not retrofit approved openings or import power systems.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M09 — Concrete next task
Source: S06 chapter 2 example; S02 stage objectives.
Transfer: Translate premise into a bounded goal with an observable constraint; demonstrate the cost of failure through relevant events.
Acceptance: Reader can state the next action, why it matters and what can go wrong.
Boundary: No invented deadline, gratuitous disaster or forced urgency in every chapter.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M10 — Demonstrate the repeatable story engine
Source: S06 chapter 3 example.
Transfer: Show a character using an established strength, gaining limited information or opportunity and encountering a new constraint. The result sets up a next experiment.
Acceptance: Technique, actual action, outcome, limitation and next objective are distinguishable.
Boundary: No cheat, guaranteed win or unlimited progression.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M11 — Earned anticipation
Source: S01/S02 monetized chapters; S04 suspense and pacing.
Transfer: Complete a meaningful local beat while leaving a specific consequential question, decision or opportunity open when appropriate.
Acceptance: Reader can identify a genuine unresolved thread; vary closure forms and pay off promises within reasonable narrative scope.
Boundary: No obligatory cliffhanger, withholding known facts from POV, or withholding a deserved resolution for payment.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M12 — Information at point of need
Source: S04 rejection observations; S06 chapter analysis.
Transfer: Explain specialist information when it changes the stakes or decision. Cut exposition that the present scene does not need.
Acceptance: A nonplayer can follow the consequence; experienced player can assess verified mechanical claim.
Boundary: No artificial dialogue solely to deliver a rulebook or recycled example.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M13 — Packaging promise alignment
Source: S01/S02 title and introduction; S04 title/synopsis; S06 rankings.
Transfer: Make title, cover and short reader blurb convey the actual distinctive experience and protagonist stakes.
Acceptance: Test title/blurb comprehension and click intent with consented readers; preserve author-selected branding unless separately approved.
Boundary: No unsupported bestseller claims or false genre tags.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M14 — Editor-facing causal outline
Source: S05 Story Outline.
Transfer: Privately present premise, major chronological changes and arc outcomes rather than using teaser copy.
Acceptance: Each arc states key causes, decisions and consequences; editor can understand the plan without reading chapters.
Boundary: No private spoilers in public Git; outline may evolve; no fabricated future result.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M15 — Named decision-centered cast profiles
Source: S05 Main Characters.
Transfer: Use name, situation, motives, relevant history, flaw, role and developmental direction for key people; prioritize protagonist and relevant supporting cast.
Acceptance: No unexplained names or appearance-only sketches; select relevant characters, not arbitrary cast dump.
Boundary: Do not treat four-character suggestion as formal cap.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M16 — Specific differentiation
Source: S05 Wow Factor; S04 first successful book; S06 CE tags.
Transfer: Specify intended audience and repeatable benefit, compare against actual relevant alternatives and distinguish incumbent readership from debut discovery.
Acceptance: One concise, concrete claim supported by sample scenes and dated comparisons; genre tags match actual content.
Boundary: Do not assert uniqueness or market demand without research.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M17 — Three-pass reading
Source: S06 Step 2 analysis.
Transfer: Reader pass identifies events and emotional questions; editor pass identifies appeal, pacing and accessibility; author pass abstracts causes, tradeoffs and reusable functions.
Acceptance: Maintain source locator, observation versus inference, original transfer, counterexample and validation scope for each mechanism.
Boundary: No copying plots, language or distinctive character expression.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M18 — Reader feedback loop
Source: S01/S02 reader interaction; S04 market awareness.
Transfer: Invite relevant, non-spoiler questions and collect feedback about comprehension, attachment and pacing. Compare recurring concerns with observable reading behavior.
Acceptance: Record cohort/source/date, question, response count, theme and action taken; preserve dissent and author judgment.
Boundary: No fake engagement, disclosure of private reader data or automatic canon changes.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M19 — Application quality and revision gate
Source: S03 contract outcomes; S05 application guidance.
Transfer: Treat a contract application as a scarce editorial opportunity. Prepare distinct synopsis/outline, relevant named profiles, evidence-backed differentiation and truthful platform history; respond to feedback before resubmission.
Acceptance: Check eligibility, current live terms, rights clearance, completeness and material revisions; author approves actual submission.
Boundary: No automatic submission or signing; deadlines and attempt limits are dated claims.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.

## WNG-M20 — Commercial fit versus artistic quality
Source: S04 paid female-lead criteria; S06 motivation and MGS discussion.
Transfer: Separate platform-specific selection, genre fit, reader demand and artistic evaluation. Define audience and income as hypotheses.
Acceptance: Track actual observed engagement and current contractual conditions; no extrapolation from FL romance to sports fiction.
Boundary: No forced romance, promised income, guaranteed signing or claims of market proof.
Status: candidate; no new reader test.
