# WNG — serial editorial protocol
Apply before drafting and during editorial review; existing canon and approved prose remain unchanged. The following adds decision prompts, not rigid quotas or guaranteed commercial outcomes.

## Opening and early progression
Reader contract: contemporary competitive sports fiction, not supernatural progression or romance-first. An ordinary protagonist has a recognizable constraint and a specific competitive ambition. Establish what he can already do, the limits of that skill and why the next opportunity matters. Treat the pilot's planned first three main chapters as a sequence: establish promise and stakes, make the next constraint actionable, show an independent perspective and credible future progress. The already approved chapters and supplement are not reopened. Verify actual delivery against the private archive before claiming a hook audit passed. Quiet scenes can carry consequences and deserve varied closure; supplement is not a substitute for main plot progression.

## Arc and scene record
For each brief identify the overarching goal, stage objective, POV immediate goal, opposition/constraint, change, unresolved question if any, and source-supported mechanism selected by function. Give supporting characters independent goals and maintain dated commitments. Escalation is earned through access, competence, costs and relationships, not automatic stronger antagonists. A scene can change a financial, competitive, emotional or social state without a dramatic twist. Do not use manufactured hostility, compulsory romantic subplot, fake cliffhanger or a cheat system.

## Review from three perspectives
Reader: what happened, what matters, what is clear, which future question arises? Editor: which promise is delivered, pacing, accessibility, state change, and actual evidence for each defect? Author: which structural choice creates the effect, what alternatives exist, which source abstraction is applicable, and what must remain original? Record observations and hypotheses separately; do not invent reader reactions. Use existing private originality and competitive validation before acceptance. Obtain actual reader evidence using the consented panel rather than grading commercial appeal as fact.

## Market research
Track separate long-term popularity and short-window sales/visibility where accessible. Compare comparable works' first successful launch rather than assuming an established author's later audience is reproducible. Record date, category, window, source, visible metrics and unknown denominator. Evaluate title, blurb, opening and actual appeal; CE-like internal labels describe audience/promise (competitive TCG, sports progression, team dynamics, grounded economic stakes), not claims of official platform tags. No invented rankings or copying premises. Use MARKET_VALIDATION for samples and thresholds. This intake did not perform live market research.

## Reader interaction
Questions may invite opinions about a decision, character preference or comprehension without spoiling future events. Author notes should be concise, truthful and optional; no pressure to pay or promise a specific plot in return for votes. Classify feedback as comprehension, emotional engagement, pacing, technical accuracy or preference; seek repeated evidence and author approval for changes. Retain diverse opinions.
