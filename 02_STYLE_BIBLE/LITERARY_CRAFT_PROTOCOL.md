## Workshop operations (candidate mechanisms, not twelve newly certified independent techniques)
Each ID gives operation, practical test and failure condition. Source mappings and actual evidence limitations are in 01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/LITERARY_CRAFT_REFERENCES.md. Private full Literary Workshop 01 remains in Library as TLD_PRIVATE_LITERARY_WORKSHOP_v1.md; the four original comparative exercises are noncanon, not reusable phrasing or approved facts.

- LRF01 Free indirect discourse (R02/R05): allow viewpoint-specific judgment within narration without thought tags. Test whether it belongs to that character and can be wrong; distinguish perception from fact. Avoid omnipresent ironic voice or italicized monologue.
- LRF02 Discrepant perception (R01/R03): people assign different significance to the same encounter. Test that each has plausible independent concerns; no unearned access to another mind or contrived misunderstanding.
- LRF03 Subtext through activity (R03): a practical action has literal purpose and emotional consequence. Test whether the reader can infer relational tension without the narrator decoding it; avoid making every gesture a cipher.
- LRF04 Object as accumulated experience (R02): useful things acquire emotional association through established history. Test literal function and traceable change in significance; no compulsory callbacks or declared symbolism.
- LRF05 Ritual and deviation (R01): ordinary pleasure develops a baseline; later variation expresses change. Test earlier intrinsic value and causal departure; no obligatory tragedy or ominous repetition.
- LRF06 Associative interior movement (R04): perception leads naturally to memory, desire, prejudice or distraction. Test character-specific associative transitions and reader orientation; avoid decorative inventories and arbitrary digression.
- LRF07 Emotional time dilation (R05/R04): expand an unusually significant brief experience while surrounding life continues. Test that extra space reveals changed attention; avoid repeated explanations, purple prose and clock logs.
- LRF08 Dramatic ellipsis (R01/R06; editorial extrapolation): omit intermediate activity when consequences make the change intelligible. Test chronology, knowledge and causal gap; never conceal required facts or manufacture canon.
- LRF09 Contradictory interiority (R02/R03): incompatible desires coexist and influence attention or action. Test credible simultaneous impulses without tidy diagnosis or immediate resolution.
- LRF10 Character-bound imagery (R01/R04): comparison emerges from particular experience rather than generic poetic vocabulary. Test specificity and functional contribution; literal language is preferable when clearer.
- LRF11 Scene-scale modulation (R04; craft synthesis): vary summary, scene, pause, expanded perception and aftermath. Test allocation against experiential importance; never compress an earned reward solely for pacing.
- LRF12 Recontextualization without exposition (R06/R05): later experience transforms the meaning of prior evidence. Test that established history survives and new scene works on its own; avoid retroactive secrets, forced prophecy or recapitulation.

## Execution within the existing production chain
Preflight: retrieve exact current authorized archive and protected state under continuity/NDM. Identify scene's human purpose, focal attention, existing motifs, independent motives, relevant source evidence and actual artistic opportunity; choose operations organically or justify plain treatment. Apply all earlier directives and scoped technical gates.
Composition: draft privately from lived event. For an emotionally flat or pivotal unapproved passage, produce two materially different treatments with the same protected facts (e.g. compressed external rendering vs close interior experience, different dialogue/subtext or temporal scale). Do not mechanically generate two versions of every paragraph. Evaluate without planning notes; retain the version or combination that adds lived specificity, tension, pleasure, ambiguity, meaning or earned change. Record rejected treatment and why. A quiet direct scene can be strongest.
Editorial: read clean passage for experiential effect, character agency, focal attention, rhythm, subtext, redundant interpretation, imagery, emotional timing, ordinary life and cumulative meaning. Cite exact private passage locations, before/after comparisons where revised and remaining defects. Distinguish judgment from measured reader reaction. Do not score technique density or length as quality. Run existing exact-version diagnostics, continuity, technical and originality checks after any changes. No automatic acceptance.
Closeout: only after explicit author approval distill exact final prose into abstract craft decisions under NDM; retire distinctive expressions, link candidate IDs and prior uses, update private continuity/usage and verified archive. Preserve approved versions; no retroactive edits without explicit revision authority. Track rolling and arc-level experiential variety and earned payoffs alongside existing serial review. Reader feedback is optional, genuine and distinct from model review.
