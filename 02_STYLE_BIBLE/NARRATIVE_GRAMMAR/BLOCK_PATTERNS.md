# Biblioteca de bloques narrativos — ejemplos originales

Los bloques son ejercicios independientes, no escenas sucesivas ni canon. En inglés para la futura edición comercial. Fundamentos: WRITING_METHOD.md y semantic/SEMANTIC_METHOD.md; los ejemplos se escribieron para este proyecto, no se extrajeron del EPUB. Cada secuencia es una opción, no fórmula obligatoria.

## B01 Apertura competitiva — restricción y objetivo (P25,P03,P11)
Estado visible → problema inmediato → elección.
 
The registration sheet was taped to the shop window. Adrian read it through the glass while the owner finished unlocking the door.

Sixteen places. Sixteen names.

His was written underneath, in a different color.

“Someone might drop,” the owner said.

Adrian checked the time. His shift started in three hours. He could wait, or he could go home with enough time to eat before work.

He took the chair nearest the door.

Control: la apertura establece objetivo, incertidumbre y coste; no anticipar el resultado.

## B02 Tensión táctica — hipótesis, alternativa, contrajuego (P01,P03,P09,P06)
Estado público → interpretación → opción descartada → decisión → respuesta → hipótesis revisada.

Marcus had played cautiously in their first two games. Adrian had come to expect the same rhythm.

This time Marcus was willing to leave a battlefield undefended.

Adrian looked at the resources still available to both players. Taking the opportunity would put pressure on Marcus, but it would also consume the resources Adrian had intended to keep for the following turn.

He stopped reaching for his cards.

Across the table, Marcus waited.

Adrian chose to preserve his options. A turn later, the position had changed enough that he could finally see what the opening had offered Marcus: a chance to dictate where the next exchange would take place.

The first decision had bought Adrian time. He still needed a way to use it.

Control: ejemplo conceptual, no registro de jugadas validado; insertar acciones legales exactas desde el motor antes de canonizar. Evitar certificar intención privada de Marcus: la última interpretación pertenece a Adrian.

## B03 Explicación técnica — error, prueba, nueva decisión (P24,P18,P04)
Pregunta concreta → contraste entre registros → prueba → nueva tarea.

“Why did you keep that hand?”

Adrian brought up the recording. He had answered the same question three times that evening, and every answer had sounded better before Nora pressed play.

She stopped the video.

“Here. What did you think I would do?”

“Commit early.”

“And when I didn't?”

He looked at the frozen table. He had spent so much time preparing for the opening he expected that he hadn't considered what he would do against a slower one.

Nora rewound the recording.

“Try answering the question again.”

Control: el lector aprende mediante análisis de una decisión; no inventar una regla o una carta.

## B04 Diálogo y subtexto económico (P22,P15,P21)
Intereses incompatibles → evasión → revelación indirecta → cambio de relación.

“Did you book the room?”

“I'm waiting for the prices to come down.”

Nora set her phone beside his. The hotel they had discussed was sold out.

Adrian closed the browser.

“I can take the morning bus.”

“It's a four-hour trip.”

“I know.”

She looked at the spreadsheet he had been editing. There was a column for the entry fee, another for transport. Nothing for accommodation.

“Send me the dates,” she said. “I'll check with my cousin.”

Control: evitar salvación automática; Nora ofrece investigar, no resolver mágicamente el dinero. El motivo de la evasión se confirma mediante contexto posterior.

## B05 Caracterización por conducta (P19,P20)
Elección bajo presión → trabajo observable → consecuencia social.

Adrian had prepared an explanation for every loss. A poor draw, an unfamiliar matchup, a decision that would have worked against anyone else.

Marcus never asked for an explanation.

He asked for the recording.

On Monday he marked three decisions. On Tuesday he brought a different list. By Thursday Adrian could predict which question he would ask before the video had even stopped.

“What else could you have done?”

At the end of the session, Adrian opened the next recording without waiting to be asked.

Control: cambio conductual pequeño y acumulativo; no declarar transformación psicológica completa a partir de un gesto.

## B06 Rival con agencia independiente (P08,P16)
Objetivo propio → alternativas → decisión → consecuencia para terceros.

Nora had received the invitation on Wednesday. It was the first testing group that had asked for her specifically, rather than for the deck she owned.

They met on the same evenings as Adrian's group.

She read the message again.

There was nothing stopping her from attending both once in a while, but she knew what would happen when tournament season started. Everyone would want the same hours.

She accepted the invitation and opened a second conversation.

Adrian deserved to hear it from her before he saw the team announcement.

Control: Nora actúa por objetivo propio. Mantener consecuencias en capítulos siguientes.

## B07 Descripción funcional (P31,P12)
Objeto concreto → acción → implicación humana.

The rubber band around Adrian's deck box had lost its elasticity. He twisted it twice before putting the box in his backpack.

The store sold replacements beside the register. He had looked at them every week since the corner split.

“Want one?” the owner asked.

Adrian glanced at the entry fee on the counter.

“Next time.”

Control: la descripción caracteriza mediante elección. No reiterar con una explicación de pobreza.

## B08 Transición de preparación (P32,P04)
Intervalo condensado → evidencia de progreso → problema nuevo.

By Friday they had played the same matchup twenty times. Adrian no longer lost for the reasons written at the top of Nora's notebook.

He had found new reasons.

They spent the last evening testing a version of the deck that neither of them particularly liked.

When the store closed, Nora put a check mark beside the first problem.

There were four more underneath.

Control: progreso parcial, no solución instantánea. Registrar qué problema cambió.

## B09 Revelación retrospectiva (P06,P28)
Pista sembrada → contexto nuevo → interpretación verificable → efecto.

Earlier scene: Marcus asked whether anyone had saved the recordings of their losses. He took copies before leaving.

Later scene: Nora opened the folder he'd shared. The files were arranged by matchup, with a short note beside each one. There were no recordings of his victories.

She had assumed that Marcus was trying to prove his list worked.

The contents suggested a different question: where did it fail?

She added her own recording to the folder.

Control: el lector dispone de la pista anterior; interpretación de Nora se mantiene como inferencia y produce una acción.

## B10 Derrota y cierre emocional (P27,P29)
Resultado → consecuencia concreta → modificación de relación.

Adrian finished ninth. The standings remained on the screen while the eight players who had qualified gathered around the front table.

Marcus was among them.

Adrian packed his cards slowly, giving himself enough time to decide what he was going to say.

“Good luck,” he told him.

Marcus thanked him, then held out his notebook.

“Could you watch the first round? I want to know what you think.”

Adrian looked at the door. His bus was due in twelve minutes.

He took the notebook.

Control: el resultado permanece; la relación abre una decisión futura. No convertir la derrota en triunfo automático.

## B11 Cierre tranquilo (P29)
Actividad concluida → gesto significativo → descanso.

The shop owner counted the chairs before locking up.

Adrian carried two of them inside without being asked. Nora held the door.

They had spent most of the evening disagreeing about the list. On the pavement she handed him the notebook anyway.

“Tomorrow?”

He nodded.

Control: capítulo puede terminar con estabilidad ganada, sin suspense artificial.

## B12 Cierre por compromiso (P30)
Opción preservada → decisión concreta → obligación futura.

The team application required a name, a contact number and a list of previous results.

Adrian had filled in the first two fields hours ago.

He opened his match history and entered every result, including the tournaments where he had dropped early.

Then he submitted it.

Control: cierre modifica estado institucional y compromete una reacción posterior.

## Arquitectura de tensión reutilizable
Anclaje (qué quiere) → ventaja inicial o expectativa → obstáculo real → reducción de opciones → decisión → contrajuego autónomo → resultado → actualización del conocimiento, recursos o relación. No toda escena requiere todos los pasos; alternar tensión, descanso y consecuencias.

## Antipatrones con reparación
- «He was incredibly nervous, terrified and anxious.» Reparación: escoger comportamiento específico y efecto, por ejemplo llegar tarde a verificar un detalle relevante.
- «Everyone knew he was a genius.» Reparación: una prueba de habilidad, una reacción justificada y un rival que pueda refutarla.
- «The opponent had the perfect counter in hand.» Reparación: limitar al estado visible o cambiar POV de manera explícita.
- «He suddenly remembered an incredible strategy.» Reparación: establecer preparación, identificar restricción y mostrar cómo decide.
- «Something unbelievable was about to happen.» Reparación: cerrar sobre una consecuencia concreta o una pregunta dramática genuina.
- Explicar tres veces la misma jugada mediante narrador, comentarista y rival: asignar a cada perspectiva una contribución diferente.
