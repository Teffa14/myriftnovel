# CFA-V01 deep pass — longitudinal mechanics
Four extracted candidates/variants, two new original blocks. Noncanon; no reader tests. Source: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/CFA/V01_SEMANTIC_DEEP.md and archive scene map. Evidence sampled at explicit separators; no full semantic certification.
 
## C09 — Retrospective explanation with return anchor
Level L3/L4. Evidence CH02 S01–S02; CH04 S03–S07. Structure: unresolved present question → explicit rewind → prior choice or evidence → return to present with changed interpretation. Conditions: reader can locate both times and causal relevance. Expected effect: delayed explanation and clarity. Example: The organizer's refusal puzzled Adrian until he remembered the request he had sent on Monday. Failure: unexplained time jump or flashback unrelated to present choice. Control: timestamp/anchor, preserve information order, check causal link. Related M84/M56.
 
## C10 — Evidence-driven allocation change
Level L3. Evidence CH04 S05–S06. Structure: test with uncertain utility → observable capability → update budget/time → future evaluation. Conditions: decision-maker controls resource and cost recorded. Expected effect: make experimentation materially consequential. Example: After watching the recording, Nora reserved another practice session for the new approach instead of immediately buying more cards. Failure: one proxy treated as guaranteed return. Control: explicit expense or schedule, opportunity cost, limits of evidence. Related M60/M89/M132.
 
## C11 — Parallel response with delayed chronological alignment
Level L4/L5. Evidence CH04 S17; CH05 S01–S04. Structure: common event → separate agents act on different subgoals → narrate partial outcome → return to shared trigger → second response. Conditions: independent objectives, shared chronology, separate POV knowledge. Expected effect: distributed agency and suspense. Example: While Adrian waited for a reply from the organizer, the store owner was already arranging a backup venue. Failure: two outcomes depend on knowledge never communicated. Control: timestamp each action and reconcile state changes. Related M52/M87; variant of parallel threads, not standalone certified mechanism.
 
## C12 — Retrospective motivation at a pressure point
Level L2/L3. Evidence CH07 S02–S04. Structure: present high-stakes choice → brief prior motivation → return to immediate choice with clarified stakes. Conditions: prior detail relevant and time shift explicit. Expected effect: emotional interpretation without exposition detached from action. Example: Before he answered, Marcus remembered the player who had lent him his first tournament deck. Failure: backstory interrupts a time-critical choice without narrative justification. Control: keep length proportional, no fabricated memory, return to current consequence. Related G10/M88.
 
## CB05 — Parallel agency
The organizer had warned the group that the room might become unavailable. Adrian took that as a reason to postpone practice.
 
Across town, Nora was checking which players could host a smaller session. She found two volunteers before he finished his shift.
 
When he finally opened the group chat, the evening's plan had already changed. He asked which deck he should bring.
 
Function: C11/M87. Shared trigger, independent action, explicit communication. No guarantee of future availability.
 
## CB06 — An experiment changes an allocation
Leah had booked two hours to review the team's losses. The first recording exposed a mistake they had also made the previous week.
 
She closed the other files.
 
“Let's spend the next hour on this one situation.”
 
Marcus looked at the remaining recordings. There would be less time for them tonight, but at least they could leave with a question answered.
 
Function: C10/M60. An observed issue justifies changing resource allocation; resolution not yet proved.
