## CB01 — Meta y adaptación
Patterns: C01/M84.

The old list was still on the wall behind the counter. Adrian recognized almost every card from the videos he had watched when he started playing.

Nora set a newer list beside it.

“Same plan?”

“Some of it. But look at what people have been bringing lately.”

He opened the recent results. The question was no longer whether the old list had worked. It was which parts of it still deserved a place.

Function: Historia relevante, cambio de entorno y pregunta verificable.

## CB02 — Interrupción cotidiana
Patterns: C02/G05.

Adrian had spent the entire bus ride planning his conversation with the team. He knew how he would explain the budget, the schedule and the changes he wanted to make.

When he walked into the shop, Nora handed him a broom.

“Meeting's in ten minutes. We need this room first.”

He put the notebook down. His first contribution to the season would apparently be removing last night's confetti.

Function: Contraste de escala, cooperación y objetivo cotidiano.

## CB03 — Interpretaciones divergentes
Patterns: C04/C07.

Marcus thought Leah had ignored his suggestion because she disliked the matchup. He mentioned it again while they were packing up.

“Which suggestion?”

He showed her the message. It had never reached the group; he had saved it as a draft.

Leah read the notes. “Actually, this is worth testing.”

Marcus added it to the schedule without mentioning the argument he had rehearsed on the way to the store.

Function: Divergencia informativa, corrección y consecuencia; evita malentendido artificial.

## CB04 — Responsabilidad y coste
Patterns: C05/C06.

The organizer offered the team the last two tables for an extra hour. Nora looked at the waiting list. Two beginners had reserved them for the next session.

“We can review the recordings somewhere else,” she said.

Adrian packed his deck. They would lose a practice game, but the group would still have the evening to review what they had learned.

Function: Elección con coste y agencia; no garantizar rendimiento.
