## TLDV002 — Redundancy tested by repeated disruption (variant_of M58; L3)
Function: an observed vulnerability prompts a redundant component that preserves an objective under a later disruption. Order: single failure → independent redundancy → comparable disturbance → limited resilience. Conditions: redundancy has a real cost; do not treat it as universal insurance. Failure: duplicating the same scene or promising success irrespective of context.
New example: The coordinator printed a spare sign-in sheet; when the tablet failed, registration continued.
