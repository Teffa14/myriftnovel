# GED-CH01 — humor, voz y relaciones
Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/GED/CH001_ANALYSIS.md. Status extracted, noncanon. Ten candidate variants/specializations, not ten certified independent techniques. Expected effect editorial hypothesis. No reader test.

## G01 — Promesa retrospectiva limitada
Level: L2. Evidence: B01.
Structure: resultado futuro sugerido → estado inicial incompatible → pregunta.
Original example: The team would remember that afternoon for years. At the time, Adrian was simply looking for somewhere to sit.
Failure/control: No prometer resultado que el arco no resuelva; variante M01/M38.

## G02 — Inventario específico como remate cómico
Level: L2. Evidence: B02.
Structure: pregunta inocente → lista concreta de consecuencias → reacción breve.
Original example: “Was it a difficult afternoon?” “We lost two hours, one extension cord and the only key to the storage room.”
Failure/control: No añadir cifras falsas; el humor depende de precisión y escala.

## G03 — Cortesía formal con contenido crítico
Level: L2. Evidence: B07.
Structure: tratamiento respetuoso → evaluación directa incompatible → réplica.
Original example: “Excellent timing,” Nora said. “You arrived just after the work was finished.”
Failure/control: Controlar relación, tono y contexto; no hacer del desprecio una norma.

## G04 — Literalidad frente a expresión idiomática
Level: L2. Evidence: B07.
Structure: expresión figurada → interpretación literal → aclaración → reacción.
Original example: “We need a miracle.” Marcus checked the schedule. “We have two hours.”
Failure/control: La divergencia nace de voz y objetivo, no de caricatura cultural; relacionado M04.

## G05 — Reacción interna contradice respuesta externa
Level: L2. Evidence: B02/B07.
Structure: respuesta social → pensamiento privado discordante → continuación.
Original example: “Of course I can manage,” Adrian said. He had already misplaced the instructions.
Failure/control: POV limitado; no leer pensamientos ajenos; relacionado M70.

## G06 — Alivio revertido por evidencia material
Level: L2/L3. Evidence: B03.
Structure: beneficio aparente → detalle incompatible → reinterpretación.
Original example: The practice room was larger than Adrian expected. Then he noticed the notice announcing its closure.
Failure/control: La pista cambia realmente la situación; variante M79/M107.

## G07 — Reputación previa demostrada por trato ajeno
Level: L3. Evidence: B06.
Structure: expectativa social → conducta observable de terceros → revisión de imagen propia.
Original example: Adrian greeted the regulars. Two of them checked whether the organizer was nearby before answering.
Failure/control: No inferir motivo como certeza; no copiar identidad heredada.

## G08 — Reprimenda convertida en responsabilidad concreta
Level: L3. Evidence: B08–B09.
Structure: desacuerdo → explicación de daño específico → elección de reparación.
Original example: Marcus asked why the owner was upset. Nora showed him the booking they had canceled without warning.
Failure/control: No resolver reparación antes de acción; relacionado M35/M77.

## G09 — Problema humano convertido en hipótesis técnica
Level: L3. Evidence: B09–B10.
Structure: necesidad ajena → conocimiento pertinente → restricciones → propuesta comprobable.
Original example: The beginners needed more practice time, so Leah began looking at how the tables were allocated.
Failure/control: No garantizar éxito; variante M57/M88/M60.

## G10 — Giro de humor a emoción por asociación
Level: L2. Evidence: B06.
Structure: interacción leve → detalle afectivo → recuerdo pertinente → cambio de conducta.
Original example: Adrian joked about the old jacket until he noticed the name stitched inside it. He folded it carefully and put it back.
Failure/control: No forzar trauma ni explicar dos veces; relacionado M51/M88.

## GB01 — Contraste de voces
Patterns: G02/G03/G05.

“How did the session go?” Adrian asked.

Nora looked up from the attendance sheet.

“Three people came. Two had brought the wrong deck. The third was you.”

“That's a promising start.”

“It was a start.”

Adrian opened the group calendar. He would have to send clearer instructions next time.

Function: La crítica cómica conduce a una decisión práctica.

## GB02 — Literalidad con objetivos distintos
Patterns: G04/G03.

Marcus closed the event registration page.

“We're going to need a bigger boat.”

Leah kept counting the names on the sheet.

“Two more tables should do it.”

He glanced at her. She had already started calling the shop.

Perhaps two tables would do it.

Function: Diferenciar registro y competencia, sin degradar a nadie.

## GB03 — Responsabilidad social
Patterns: G06/G07/G08.

Adrian arrived at the store expecting to find the practice group waiting for him. The owner was stacking chairs instead.

“They left twenty minutes ago.”

He checked his phone. He had sent the wrong starting time.

“Can we book another night?”

“Ask them first.”

He put the phone down and began helping with the chairs.

Function: Error, consecuencia y reparación pendiente.

## GB04 — Problema antes de solución
Patterns: G09/G10.

Nora watched the newcomers leave after the second round. Several had been sharing a single practice deck.

She asked the owner how often that happened.

“Most Saturdays.”

At home she took out the inventory of cards the group rarely used. There might be enough to assemble another legal list, but she would have to check the format and ask the owners.

She sent the group a question, not a shopping list.

Function: Necesidad, hipótesis, restricciones y siguiente acción.
