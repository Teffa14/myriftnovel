# Extensión de gramática — MSC-EX01
Ocho mecanismos candidatos y tres bloques originales. Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/OPENING_EXCERPT_ANALYSIS.md. Estos son ejercicios independientes, no canon ni reproducción de la escena fuente.

## M01 — Anomalía concreta antes de la explicación
Expectativa cotidiana → detalle verificable incompatible → reacción.
Example: The pairings had been posted ten minutes earlier. Adrian found his name beside a table he had never played at. The opponent column was blank.
Use: abrir con información legible que requiere una decisión; no acumular misterios decorativos.

## M02 — Hipótesis provisional
Evidencia incompleta → explicación plausible → dato que la debilita.
Example: He assumed the store had changed the schedule. Then he noticed that every other match was starting on time.
Use: permitir error razonable sin volver al protagonista ingenuo.

## M03 — Pregunta interrumpida
Pregunta iniciada → acción de otro personaje → prioridad nueva.
Example: “Did you get a chance to—” Nora stopped speaking when the judge called her name.
Use: la interrupción debe cambiar la acción, no servir únicamente como suspenso.

## M04 — Contraste de registros
Afirmación institucional → lectura cotidiana → respuesta práctica.
Example: The coordinator called it a logistical adjustment. Adrian looked at the three train tickets on his phone. It was an expensive adjustment.
Use: humor ligado a costes reales y perspectiva, sin humillar al interlocutor.

## M05 — Reacción breve y específica
Dato nuevo → pausa → pregunta o gesto que revela implicación.
Example: “The qualifier is tomorrow.” Adrian folded the testing schedule in half. “Tomorrow?”
Use: variar con acciones y silencios; evitar puntuación exagerada.

## M06 — Exposición por interrogatorio motivado
Necesidad de información → pregunta concreta → respuesta parcial → nueva decisión.
Example: “Can I change my list?” “Registration closed at noon.” He checked the submission receipt before asking about the appeal process.
Use: comprobar reglamento del evento antes de canonizar; información necesaria, no exposición enciclopédica.

## M07 — Deducción por convergencia
Dos evidencias independientes → hipótesis limitada → comprobación.
Example: Marcus had missed two testing sessions, and his seat on the travel booking had disappeared. Nora called him before assuming he had left the team.
Use: indicios permiten hipótesis, no certeza sobre motivos privados.

## M08 — Revelación que redefine el problema
Pregunta local → respuesta comprobable → nueva escala de conflicto.
Example: Adrian had come to ask why his payment had been returned. The organizer turned the laptop toward him. The event itself had been canceled.
Use: resolver una pregunta real; dar consecuencias y evitar cliffhanger genérico.

## MB01 — Descubrimiento de una restricción institucional
Adrian found his name on the waiting list. He checked the confirmation email again. It was dated Tuesday.

“Did you receive my payment?” he asked.

The organizer searched the registration system. “Yes. You registered for the Sunday event.”

Adrian looked at the banner over the entrance. Saturday's qualifier. The two events had been advertised on the same page.

“Can I move the registration?”

“If someone drops.”

He had paid for a seat. Just not the seat he needed.

Function: expectativa, anomalía, explicación provisional, confirmación y coste. Evento ficticio; comprobar políticas antes de canonizar.

## MB02 — Humor de personaje y exposición funcional
“It's an administrative fee,” Marcus said.

Nora looked at the invoice. “For what?”

“Processing the entry.”

She opened the payment confirmation. “Apparently my entry has a demanding social life.”

Marcus pulled his chair closer. They went through the charges together, separating the fee for the event from the cost of the payment service.

By the time they finished, Nora had decided to register directly at the store next time.

Function: humor por contraste de registro; la explicación altera una elección. No afirmar políticas reales sin fuente.

## MB03 — Deducción estratégica con información imperfecta
The opening had looked familiar. Adrian recognized three cards from the list Marcus had shared on Monday.

The fourth was new.

He checked the notes from their previous session. Marcus had complained about that exact matchup, and the new card might have been intended to address it.

Adrian put the notebook away. A plausible explanation was enough to reconsider his plan, but it was not enough to know what else Marcus had changed.

He would have to learn that during the match.

Function: reconocimiento, evidencia contradictoria, hipótesis y decisión bajo incertidumbre. No revelar mano oculta ni resultado. Partida conceptual no validada.

## Selector
M01–M03 para aperturas y cambios de situación; M04–M05 para voz y reacciones; M06 para exposición motivada; M07 para deducción; M08 para giros verificables. MB01–MB03 ilustran combinación, no recetas fijas. Usar una función principal y una secundaria, cambiar sintaxis y contenido; nunca insertar ejemplos literalmente en manuscrito.
