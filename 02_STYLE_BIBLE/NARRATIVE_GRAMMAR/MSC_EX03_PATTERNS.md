# MSC-EX03 — competencia demostrada, coordinación y aprendizaje
10 patrones candidatos (M19–M28) y 4 bloques originales (MB08–MB11). Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/TEAM_COMBAT_EXCERPT_ANALYSIS.md. Ejercicios independientes, no canon. No describir ayuda de compañeros durante partidas oficiales sin validar reglamento; los ejemplos de coordinación corresponden a preparación y revisión.

## M19 — Presentación de elenco mediante contribución
Estructura: persona → responsabilidad concreta → primera acción distintiva.
Example: Leah opened the matchup spreadsheet before anyone sat down. Marcus brought the decks. Nora cleared the table and asked which opening they wanted to test.
Control: cada persona adquiere agencia mediante acción; no convertir presentación en fichas biográficas.

## M20 — Desconfianza con causa
Estructura: expectativa del grupo → evidencia limitada → reserva razonable.
Example: Leah had seen plenty of impressive local results. What she had not seen was Adrian play the matchup they needed to prepare.
Control: el escéptico puede tener razón; el narrador no debe castigarlo por dudar.

## M21 — Fracaso que delimita competencia
Estructura: intento → restricción → resultado negativo → cambio de conducta.
Example: Adrian lost the first three practice games. On the fourth, he stopped defending his original plan and asked Leah to explain where the position had begun to deteriorate.
Control: preservar pérdidas y consecuencias; no reescribir el fracaso como victoria secreta.

## M22 — Observación activa
Estructura: acción de experto → detalle elegido por observador → pregunta útil.
Example: Marcus changed his opening plan twice during the session. Adrian marked both games and asked what information had prompted the change.
Control: una observación no demuestra intención privada; comprobarla después.

## M23 — Sinergia secuencial
Estructura: acción preparatoria → condición creada → segunda acción aprovecha condición → consecuencia.
Example: The early exchange had reduced the opponent's available options. Only then did Nora consider the line she had been saving for a more favorable position.
Control: plantilla conceptual, no jugada validada. Confirmar efecto, timing, recursos y respuestas legales.

## M24 — Compresión de repetición
Estructura: varias pruebas similares → resultado agregado → anomalía que merece escena.
Example: They repeated the opening six times. Five games ended with the same problem. Nora kept the recording of the sixth.
Control: no inventar resultados de testing; mostrar la excepción solo si existe.

## M25 — Descripción que prepara una regla
Estructura: detalle visible → diferencia relevante → implicación.
Example: Two clocks stood at the front of the room. Adrian checked which one the organizer was using before planning his break.
Control: el detalle tiene función futura verificable, no es decoración.

## M26 — Jerarquía de amenazas
Estructura: riesgos posibles → distinguir recuperable de crítico → prioridad.
Example: Leah drew a line between the errors they could recover from and those that repeatedly cost them the match. The second group was much smaller.
Control: la prioridad procede de evidencia, no de conocimiento omnisciente.

## M27 — Reformulación para demostrar comprensión
Estructura: instrucción → respuesta en palabras propias → corrección o confirmación.
Example: “So we're testing whether the opening survives, even when the rest of the list stays the same?” Adrian asked. Nora nodded and returned the spare cards to their box.
Control: no confundir comprensión con ejecución ni usar aprobación desproporcionada.

## M28 — Reconocimiento parcial y prueba pendiente
Estructura: evidencia de mejora → cambio moderado de actitud → prueba siguiente.
Example: Leah added Adrian's suggestion to the testing schedule. She still wanted results before recommending the list to anyone.
Control: confianza y reputación cambian proporcionalmente a evidencia real.

## MB08 — Entrar en un equipo sin prestigio
Adrian arrived carrying the deck he had used at his local store.

Leah asked for the matchup records. He had kept his tournament results, but none of the practice games.

“That's fine,” she said, moving an empty sheet toward him. “We can start tonight.”

Marcus glanced at the three lists they still needed to test. They had expected another experienced pilot.

Nora handed Adrian the first deck.

“Play this one for a few games. Tell us what feels different.”

Adrian sat down. He had wanted to show them what he could do with his own list. For the moment, the most useful thing he could offer was a different perspective.

Function: introducción coral, competencia aún incierta, objetivos incompatibles y tarea concreta. Mantener POV de Adrian; la expectativa grupal debe haber sido comunicada o atribuirse explícitamente a inferencia.

## MB09 — Coordinación en testing
The first recordings belonged to Marcus. He had marked the decisions that seemed to determine the outcome.

Leah checked those moments against the notes she had taken during the session. Two were worth studying; a third had occurred after the position was already difficult to recover.

Nora built the next set of practice games around the earlier decision.

Adrian watched the first one and understood why they had divided the work. Alone, he would have spent the evening changing the list. Together, they had found a question they could actually test.

Function: contribuciones complementarias → información transformada → siguiente acción colectiva. El conocimiento se construye fuera de una partida oficial.

## MB10 — Enseñar prioridades sin una enciclopedia
Nora had written five possible problems on the whiteboard.

“Which one are we losing to most often?” Adrian asked.

Leah pointed to the second. Marcus moved the marker to the fourth.

“The second happens more,” he said. “The fourth is the one we haven't recovered from.”

Adrian copied both into his notebook.

“So we need to track frequency and cost separately?”

Leah took the marker back and drew another column.

“Exactly.”

Function: reducir complejidad, distinguir frecuencia y gravedad, reformulación verificable. No afirmar causalidad por datos insuficientes.

## MB11 — Reputación ganada por etapas
Leah read Adrian's revised notes before the next testing session. He had separated what he had seen from what he thought the opponent was trying to accomplish.

She crossed out one inference and circled another.

“Let's keep this question,” she said.

Adrian reached for the deck.

“Same matchup?”

“Same matchup.”

She moved her notebook so he could see the results sheet.

They still disagreed about the list. At least now they were testing the same problem.

Function: reconocimiento moderado, colaboración y siguiente prueba. No confundir una buena observación con dominio técnico.

## Selector y restricciones
M19/M20 para incorporación y conflicto institucional; M21/M22 para aprendizaje; M23/M24 para acción y compresión; M25/M26/M27 para exposición y priorización; M28 para progreso social. MB08–MB11 ilustran composición original. Evitar atribuir conocimiento privado, repetir el mecanismo de asombro de terceros y sustituir rendimiento por aprobación verbal. Todos los ejemplos competitivos concretos deben validarse antes de publicar.
