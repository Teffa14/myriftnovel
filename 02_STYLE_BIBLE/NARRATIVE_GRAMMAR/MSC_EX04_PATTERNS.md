# MSC-EX04 — economía, objetivos paralelos y fricción social
Ocho mecanismos candidatos (M29–M36) y tres bloques originales (MB12–MB14). Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/ECONOMY_AND_AGENCY_EXCERPT_ANALYSIS.md. Ejemplos independientes, en inglés, no canon. Los precios y acuerdos son hipotéticos, no datos reales de mercado.

## M29 — Recompensa que genera una obligación
Estructura: resultado obtenido → posibilidad económica → compromiso nuevo.
Example: The store offered Leah a small fee for running its next beginner session. She looked at the dates before accepting. Two of them overlapped with testing.
Control: la oportunidad debe tener un coste o compromiso; distinguir promesa de pago efectivo.

## M30 — Valor contextual mediante pregunta
Estructura: objeto o resultado → pregunta de valor → explicación vinculada a disponibilidad o demanda.
Example: Adrian asked why two copies of the same card had sold for different prices. Nora showed him the condition notes on the listings.
Control: verificar datos, edición y condición; no inventar precio de mercado ni inferir rareza de una única venta.

## M31 — Plazo externo que reorganiza el plan
Estructura: compromiso → fecha límite → tareas y responsables → contingencia.
Example: The venue needed their final player count by Thursday. Marcus contacted the two people who had not confirmed, while Leah worked out what a smaller booking would cost.
Control: conservar fechas y responsabilidades; el plan no equivale a cumplimiento.

## M32 — Barrera de acceso y objetivo personal
Estructura: deseo de participar → requisito concreto → actividad alternativa con progreso verificable.
Example: Adrian wanted to join the advanced testing group. Its members asked for recordings of his recent matches. He had none, so he began saving them at his next local event.
Control: requisito ficticio de grupo, no regla oficial; no garantizar aceptación futura.

## M33 — Hilos simultáneos con causalidad
Estructura: objetivo colectivo activo → separación por restricción → objetivo individual relacionado.
Example: While Nora confirmed travel arrangements, Adrian stayed behind to finish the testing notes she needed before booking his seat.
Control: registrar ambos estados; no resolver la actividad fuera de escena sin evidencia.

## M34 — Ecosistema descubierto por necesidad
Estructura: necesidad concreta → acceso a institución o espacio → información funcional → nueva posibilidad.
Example: The shop had none of the cards Leah needed. Its owner gave her the date of the next trade night, where several collectors usually brought their binders.
Control: introducir lugares cuando sirven a la acción; distinguir expectativa de disponibilidad confirmada.

## M35 — Conflicto de valores mediante opciones
Estructura: solicitud → límite percibido → negativa justificada → reacción autónoma.
Example: “Could you cover my entry fee?” Marcus asked. Adrian checked the amount he had set aside for travel. “I can lend you half. I need the rest on Sunday.”
Control: mostrar razones y costes de ambos; no declarar que el protagonista tiene razón por defecto.

## M36 — Fricción seguida de posibilidad relacional
Estructura: desacuerdo → respuesta concreta → nueva propuesta → vínculo todavía abierto.
Example: Leah disagreed with the way Adrian had recorded their results. She offered to compare their notes after the next event. He asked what time she would be there.
Control: la cooperación no borra la discrepancia ni prueba amistad.

## MB12 — Economía que produce una decisión
The owner counted the unsold packs after the event. He asked Nora whether her group would be willing to teach the new players next month.

“How many sessions?”

“Three. I can pay for each one.”

Nora opened her calendar. The last date was the same evening Marcus had reserved for their final testing session.

“I can do the first two,” she said. “Let me ask about the third.”

She sent Marcus a message before leaving the shop. If the group could move its session, she would have enough to cover part of the trip.

Function: oportunidad económica → restricción temporal → coordinación → resultado abierto. No registrar ingreso hasta acuerdo y pago.

## MB13 — El mundo competitivo como recurso
Adrian had checked the online listings twice. The only copies he could afford would not arrive before Saturday.

At the shop, Leah asked him to check the trade board beside the register.

He found two names and a date for the next exchange. One of the collectors was already in the room.

“Do you have anything they're looking for?” Leah asked.

Adrian opened his binder. Until that moment, he had treated most of its contents as cards he no longer needed.

Function: problema de acceso → descubrimiento de canal → reevaluación de inventario. No garantizar trato ni precio.

## MB14 — Desacuerdo sin convertirlo en enemistad
Marcus held out the travel spreadsheet.

“Can you put the hotel on your card? We'll pay you back on Friday.”

Adrian looked at the total. His rent was due on Thursday.

“I can't cover all of that.”

Marcus took the phone back. “Even for two days?”

“Especially for those two days.”

For a moment neither spoke. Marcus reopened the booking page.

“What if we split the rooms into separate reservations?”

Adrian pulled his chair closer.

Function: dos objetivos legítimos, límite financiero, reacción, alternativa. La negociación continúa; no asumir pago ni compromiso.
