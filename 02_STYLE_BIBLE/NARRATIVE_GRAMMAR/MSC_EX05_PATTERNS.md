# MSC-EX05 — mercados, observación y aspiración
Ocho entradas candidatas (M37–M44), tres bloques originales (MB15–MB17). Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/AUCTION_EXCERPT_ANALYSIS.md. Ejemplos ingleses no canónicos. Precios y eventos hipotéticos; verificar reglas y datos cuando se incorporen al manuscrito.

## M37 — Escala mediante efecto observable
Estructura: capacidad inicial → demanda → adaptación visible.
Example: The owner had borrowed six folding tables from the café next door. By noon he was asking whether they had any more.
Control: mostrar cambio material, no inventar asistencia ni popularidad. Complementa M25.

## M38 — Información parcial con promesa concreta
Estructura: anuncio → dato disponible → dato reservado → pregunta verificable.
Example: The trade organizer had posted photographs of the collection. Prices would be added when the doors opened.
Control: la información faltante debe responder a un proceso real; evitar ocultación artificial. Complementa M01/M08.

## M39 — Experiencia demostrada por pronóstico
Estructura: predicción justificada → observación → confirmación parcial o refutación.
Example: Leah expected the cheapest copies to disappear before lunch. By the time Adrian returned, two had sold and one was still available.
Control: registrar aciertos y errores; no convertir un resultado en certeza universal. Complementa P20/M28.

## M40 — Valor por comparación longitudinal
Estructura: referencia previa → nuevo objeto comparable → diferencia relevante → interpretación.
Example: Adrian recognized the artwork from the binder he had checked on Tuesday. This copy was in better condition, but he still needed to compare the edition before judging the asking price.
Control: comparar equivalentes; verificar moneda, edición, estado y fecha. Amplía M30.

## M41 — Motivaciones distintas ante el mismo objeto
Estructura: objeto compartido → criterios de valor divergentes → conflicto o intercambio.
Example: Marcus wanted the card for his tournament list. Nora had been looking for that illustration since the set was released.
Control: dar objetivos independientes; no reducir coleccionistas a irracionalidad.

## M42 — Precio transformado en coste de oportunidad
Estructura: precio → referencia al presupuesto → alternativa sacrificada → decisión.
Example: The card cost almost as much as his return ticket. Adrian closed the listing and checked whether anyone had a copy to lend him.
Control: comprobar recursos y obligaciones; no confundir deseo con compra. Amplía M15/M35.

## M43 — Aprendizaje sin adquisición
Estructura: acceso a información → restricción material → registro → objetivo revisado.
Example: Adrian left the trade night with the same cards he had brought. His notebook contained three names, two price ranges and a question about the next release.
Control: progreso observable sin beneficio económico inventado.

## M44 — Ritual público que regula ritmo
Estructura: señal de inicio → atención colectiva → fase de intercambio → resultado concreto.
Example: Conversations faded as the organizer read the standings aloud. Adrian waited until the eighth name before looking at his own result.
Control: establecer procedimiento y ritmo; no imponer reacciones idénticas a toda la audiencia.

## MB15 — Mercado como contraste de valores
The collector turned the card over and examined the corners.

Marcus waited until she had finished.

“Would you trade it?”

“What for?”

He opened his binder and pointed to two cards from the same set. She glanced at them, then asked whether he had any of the older illustrations.

Marcus had spent the week looking for a replacement for his tournament deck. She had spent months completing a collection.

He moved to the next page of his binder.

Perhaps there was a trade they could both use.

Function: interés competitivo, interés coleccionable, negociación y posibilidad abierta. No confirmar precio ni intercambio.

## MB16 — Mentor con previsión limitada
Leah stopped beside the first table.

“Don't buy those yet,” she said. “Two other sellers brought copies this morning.”

Adrian wrote down the asking price before they moved on.

The second seller had already sold his cheapest copy. The third had one left, but its condition was worse than Adrian expected.

He returned to the first table and asked whether the price was negotiable.

Leah had been right about the supply. The rest of the decision was his.

Function: predicción comprobable, evidencia mixta y agencia del aprendiz. No asumir descuento.

## MB17 — Aspiración económica mediante restricción
Adrian had spent most of the afternoon watching people trade cards he couldn't afford.

Before leaving, he asked the store owner which cards customers requested most frequently.

The owner showed him a short list of unfilled requests and explained which had been sitting there for weeks.

Adrian copied the names. He would need to find out whether the prices justified the effort, and whether he could obtain any copies without spending the money reserved for his next event.

At home, he opened a new spreadsheet.

The first column was demand. The second was cost.

Function: observación → oportunidad → límite → hipótesis de trabajo. No garantizar margen, suministro ni éxito empresarial.
