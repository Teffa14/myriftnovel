# MSC-EX06 — ambición, élite y contraste de perspectivas
Ocho entradas candidatas M45–M52; tres bloques originales MB18–MB20. Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/AMBITION_AND_ELITE_EXCERPT_ANALYSIS.md. Ejercicios ingleses independientes, no canon, no citas. Las cantidades son ilustrativas; validar antes de publicar.

## M45 — Escala económica relativa
Estructura: importe observado → referencia a obligación personal → implicación.
Example: The entry fee was manageable. The two nights away from work were what made the trip expensive.
Control: distinguir costes directos, ingreso perdido y presupuesto. Amplía M42/P15.

## M46 — Aceleración perceptible
Estructura: ritmo establecido → variación observable → reacción o cambio de decisión.
Example: The first three places had taken a week to fill. By lunchtime on Friday, the remaining seats were gone.
Control: secuencia y tiempos comprobables; no inventar demanda. Relacionado con M37/M44.

## M47 — Aspiración revisada por evidencia
Estructura: objetivo anterior → descubrimiento → alternativa plausible → siguiente paso.
Example: Adrian had been preparing for the local circuit alone. After watching the team review its matches, he began looking for a group that would test regularly.
Control: plantear búsqueda, no éxito garantizado.

## M48 — Reputación pública frente a criterio privado
Estructura: reconocimiento externo → escena independiente → decisión que revela valores.
Example: At the convention, people kept asking Leah about her last tournament. That evening she spent an hour helping a teammate decide whether to withdraw from the next one.
Control: separar fuentes de información y POV; evitar que reputación sustituya evidencia.

## M49 — Innovación anticipada sin resultado
Estructura: problema anterior → alternativa nueva → hipótesis de utilidad → prueba pendiente.
Example: Marcus had changed his list after the last event. He wanted the others to test it against the opponent he had found hardest to prepare for.
Control: no esconder información de manera injustificada; no inventar victoria ni efecto de carta. Complementa M17.

## M50 — Rasgo cotidiano independiente del talento
Estructura: conducta habitual → consecuencia social → contraste con actividad competitiva.
Example: Leah could organize an entire testing weekend, but she always left her water bottle at the store. Nora kept a spare beside the door.
Control: evitar excentricidad ornamental o diagnóstico no sustentado.

## M51 — Contraste ambiente/estado emocional
Estructura: detalle externo → conducta o percepción focal → significado contextual.
Example: The others lingered outside the shop in the evening sun. Adrian kept checking the bus schedule, trying to work out what he would tell his manager.
Control: no reiterar emoción ya demostrada; el contraste debe servir a la escena.

## M52 — Corte de POV con información asimétrica
Estructura: escena A establece pregunta → escena B autorizada añade información distinta → retorno sin transferir conocimiento.
Example: Adrian sent the team his availability and waited. Across town, Nora and Leah were comparing the dates with their own work schedules.
Control: marcar corte; conservar ledger de conocimiento por personaje. Relacionado con M33/P06.

## MB18 — La ambición cambia de forma
Adrian had saved enough for one event out of town. He had spent weeks comparing travel costs and was nearly ready to book.

At practice, Nora asked whether he could help organize the beginner sessions the store was planning. He knew most of the regulars and had already spent several evenings explaining the game to newcomers.

“Would they pay for that?”

“The owner wants to discuss it on Monday.”

Adrian left his travel spreadsheet open when he got home. The dates still mattered. But there was another possibility worth investigating, and it did not require him to win a tournament first.

He wrote down the questions he would ask on Monday.

Function: necesidad → oportunidad → cambio de aspiración parcial → acción verificable. No confirmar empleo ni ingresos.

## MB19 — Presentación independiente de una jugadora de élite
Leah's name was printed on the event banner. At the shop, Adrian heard two players discussing the list she had used the previous weekend.

* * *

Across town, Leah was at her kitchen table with the same list in front of her.

Her teammate pointed to the notes from the final round.

“Are you keeping the changes?”

“For now. I want to see whether they solve the problem in the other matchup.”

Her phone rang. She answered it and apologized for forgetting to pick up her brother.

The cards would have to wait.

Function: fama pública → POV independiente → incertidumbre técnica y obligación personal. No afirmar superioridad ni resultado futuro.

## MB20 — Una innovación aún no demostrada
Marcus brought two versions of his list to the practice session.

“Which one are we using?” Nora asked.

“Both. I think I found a way to approach the matchup differently.”

She looked at the changes and asked him to explain what he expected to happen when the opponent played the slower opening.

He had an answer for the faster one. For the slower one, he needed more games.

Nora moved the second deck toward him.

“Then let's start there.”

Function: innovación → objeción independiente → límite de conocimiento → prueba. No especificar efecto ni jugada sin replay.
