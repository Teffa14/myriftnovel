# MSC-EX07 — aprendizaje experimental y gestión de recursos
10 entradas candidatas M53–M62, cuatro bloques originales MB21–MB24. Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/EXPERIMENTATION_EXCERPT_ANALYSIS.md. Inglés original, ejercicios no canónicos; validar precios, reglas y partidas antes de publicación.

## M53 — Mentor como acceso a conocimiento práctico
Estructura: necesidad → experiencia situada de otro → reducción de búsqueda → decisión propia.
Example: Nora knew which sellers brought singles to the weekly event. Adrian still had to decide which cards were worth buying.
Control: el mentor facilita, no resuelve todo.

## M54 — Calidad frente a capacidad de uso
Estructura: recurso costoso → comparación funcional → límite del usuario → elección.
Example: The expensive list gave Adrian more options. Until he understood when to use them, he wanted to test the version he already owned.
Control: no equiparar coste y rendimiento ni garantizar superioridad de lo barato.

## M55 — Negociación como caracterización
Estructura: oferta → restricción privada comunicada → contrapropuesta → aceptación o rechazo.
Example: “I can pay for the three copies,” Adrian said, “but only if I collect them here.” The seller checked his calendar before answering.
Control: no garantizar descuento ni justificar engaño por eficiencia.

## M56 — Recuperación de un concepto sembrado
Estructura: dato anterior memorable → explicación posterior → comprensión nueva.
Example: Adrian finally understood the note Leah had made about the final round. The opening he had dismissed was the one they were trying to reproduce.
Control: mantener ledger de pistas y no repetir la explicación completa.

## M57 — Herramienta contextual
Estructura: limitación del entorno → recurso aparentemente secundario → función necesaria.
Example: Nora brought a second notebook so the observer could record decisions without interrupting either player.
Control: utilidad depende de contexto; comprobar reglas si se usa en evento.

## M58 — Adaptación del papel a una limitación
Estructura: dificultad individual → redistribución consensuada → contribución específica.
Example: Leah could only stay for the first hour. Marcus asked whether she could review the opening games instead of playing the entire session.
Control: conservar agencia, evitar paternalismo y estereotipos.

## M59 — Progreso parcial cuantificado
Estructura: intervalo de trabajo → resultado verificable → objetivo pendiente.
Example: By closing time, they had tested two of the four matchups. Nora left the remaining pages blank.
Control: no inventar métricas ni prometer que el trabajo restante será igual.

## M60 — Experimento con presupuesto limitado
Estructura: hipótesis → insumo disponible → prueba acotada → registro.
Example: Adrian had an hour before work. Rather than rebuild the whole deck, he selected one change and wrote down what he expected it to improve.
Control: comparar condiciones; el tiempo y resultado deben ser coherentes.

## M61 — Fracaso con coste verificable
Estructura: intento → señal de fallo → pérdida concreta → diagnóstico provisional.
Example: The new list had taken most of Saturday to prepare. After the third game, Adrian realized that he had never tested the assumption behind its central change.
Control: no equiparar una derrota con refutación causal; conservar incertidumbre.

## M62 — Pausa deliberada antes de repetir
Estructura: fallo → impulso de repetir → información faltante → decisión de detenerse.
Example: He reached for the spare cards, then put them back. Before making another change, he needed to understand why the first one had failed.
Control: la pausa debe conducir a una revisión o decisión posterior, no a solución milagrosa.

## MB21 — Adquisición de recursos y aprendizaje
The seller had three copies of the card Adrian wanted.

“Are these the same edition?”

Nora waited while the seller checked the markings. Two were, and the third belonged to a different printing.

Adrian opened the list on his phone. For the next practice session, he needed only one copy.

“I'll take this one,” he said.

The other two could wait until he knew whether the change was worth keeping.

Function: pregunta técnica, presupuesto y adquisición proporcional a hipótesis. Verificar edición, precio y legalidad.

## MB22 — Un equipo adapta el trabajo
Marcus arrived twenty minutes before Leah had to leave.

They had intended to spend the evening playing a series of practice matches. Nora suggested changing the order.

Leah reviewed the opening hands while Marcus prepared the decks. Adrian recorded the situations they wanted to reproduce later.

By the time Leah left, the remaining players had a shorter list of questions and a plan for testing them.

Function: restricción individual → redistribución → contribución → progreso colectivo. No afirmar resultados de partidas no realizadas.

## MB23 — El primer diseño fracasa
Adrian had replaced six cards before the session.

By the third game, he was struggling to explain which of the changes had helped and which had caused new problems. Nora asked him to show her the original list.

He opened the document. He had overwritten it.

For the rest of the evening, they reconstructed the previous version from his notes. They had lost the time reserved for another matchup, and he still did not know whether the new list was better.

When he got home, he saved a separate copy before changing anything else.

Function: error de método → coste temporal → consecuencia técnica → cambio de conducta. El fracaso no demuestra que todas las cartas nuevas sean malas.

## MB24 — Detenerse también es una decisión
The last practice game ended shortly before the shop closed.

“Another one?” Marcus asked.

Adrian looked at the notes. They had recorded the same problem twice, but disagreed about when it began.

“Tomorrow. I want to watch these first.”

He copied the recordings to his laptop and packed the deck without changing a card.

There was no point spending another evening testing a question he had not yet managed to define.

Function: incertidumbre → rechazo de repetición inmediata → revisión programada. No confirmar diagnóstico ni solución.
