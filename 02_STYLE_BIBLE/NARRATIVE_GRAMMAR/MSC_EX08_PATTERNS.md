# MSC-EX08 — innovación infravalorada e ironía dramática
10 entradas candidatas M63–M72 y cuatro bloques originales MB25–MB28. Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/UNDERVALUE_INNOVATION_EXCERPT_ANALYSIS.md. Ejercicios originales en inglés, no canon. No implican efectos reales de cartas ni partidas validadas.

## M63 — Propiedad contextual ignorada
Estructura: valoración general desfavorable → atributo específico → situación en que podría importar.
Example: The card had performed poorly in most of their practice games. Leah wondered whether they had ever tested it against the list it was supposed to answer.
Control: no confundir una aplicación posible con superioridad demostrada.

## M64 — Mejora incremental mediante repetición
Estructura: versiones comparables → cambio observable → explicación provisional.
Example: Adrian needed less time to explain his decision in the final practice game. Nora checked whether he had also made fewer mistakes.
Control: velocidad y precisión son dimensiones distintas; no inferir causalidad sin control.

## M65 — Investigación previa a una negociación
Estructura: desconocimiento → comparables → rango esperado → decisión.
Example: Before asking for a price, Marcus checked the recent listings and made sure they referred to the same printing.
Control: distinguir precio publicado, vendido, condición y liquidez.

## M66 — Divergencia de criterios técnicos
Estructura: evaluador A prioriza un atributo → evaluador B examina otro → desacuerdo significativo.
Example: Marcus was looking for consistency across the field. Leah wanted a specific answer to the matchup that had ended her last two tournaments.
Control: ambos criterios pueden ser razonables; no declarar ganador sin pruebas.

## M67 — Negociación con unidad explícita
Estructura: oferta ambigua → aclaración de cantidad/unidad → confirmación → intercambio.
Example: “Twenty for the pair?” Nora asked. “Each,” the seller said. She checked her budget again before making a counteroffer.
Control: confirmar total, cantidad, moneda y pago; no ocultar errores contables.

## M68 — Beneficio observado frente a proyección
Estructura: transacción real → supuesto de repetición → coste omitido → revisión.
Example: He could afford the next event if he sold the remaining copies at the same price. Three listings had already been sitting unanswered for a week.
Control: no extrapolar una venta como ingreso recurrente.

## M69 — Innovación descubierta por otro personaje
Estructura: autor subestima posibilidad → especialista reconoce conexión → propone comprobarla.
Example: Leah found an interaction in the notes Adrian had discarded. She asked him to explain why he had stopped testing that version.
Control: preservar contribuciones independientes y no atribuir certeza al observador.

## M70 — Ironía dramática por corte de perspectiva
Estructura: personaje A concluye provisionalmente → personaje B conoce un dato diferente → lector comprende la brecha.
Example: Adrian removed the list from the shared testing folder. That evening Nora opened her saved copy and highlighted a matchup he had not included in his summary.
Control: corte explícito; no transferir información entre POV sin comunicación.

## M71 — Probabilidad como condición estratégica
Estructura: efecto posible → condiciones y frecuencia → valor de la decisión → riesgo residual.
Example: Even if the new opening appeared more frequently, Nora wanted to know what happened in the games where it did not.
Control: verificar probabilidades, condiciones y costes; no usar porcentajes como certezas.

## M72 — Cierre por reinterpretación incompleta
Estructura: objeto conocido → lectura nueva → pregunta concreta que requiere comprobación.
Example: Leah reread the line in Adrian's notes. He had called the result an exception. What if it was the situation they had been looking for?
Control: la pregunta se apoya en evidencia previa; resolverla con prueba posterior, no milagro.

## MB25 — Descubrimiento de una carta contextual
Marcus had removed the card from the list after their first testing session.

“It didn't help,” he told Leah.

She opened the records and checked the opponents they had played. None of the games had included the opening they had discussed when they added it.

“Then we haven't answered our question yet.”

Marcus looked at the results again.

He had a good reason to dislike the card in those games. He still did not know what it would do in the matchup they had intended it for.

They put the original question back on the testing sheet.

Function: evaluación general → ausencia de prueba relevante → reformulación. No carta ni efecto real especificado.

## MB26 — Desacuerdo entre especialistas
Adrian had ranked the changes by how often they appeared useful.

Nora read the list and marked one near the bottom.

“Why this one?”

“Because it addresses a different problem.”

She showed him the two losses she had saved from the previous weekend. He had included both in his results, but had not separated them from the rest.

They created a second column. Frequency would remain important. So would the consequence of having no answer when the situation arose.

Function: criterios distintos → evidencia → nueva medición. No afirmar que la variante es mejor.

## MB27 — Economía con continuidad
Adrian entered the sale in his spreadsheet.

He had received enough to recover the cost of the cards he had bought for testing. On the next line, he wrote the expected price for the remaining copies.

Nora pointed to the empty payment column.

“Have those sold?”

“Not yet.”

He moved the figure into a separate forecast sheet.

The money available for Saturday had not changed.

Function: ingreso realizado → proyección → corrección de estado. Mantener costes, unidades y saldos separados.

## MB28 — Ironía dramática con agencia independiente
Adrian sent the team a short report before going to work. He had marked the last variant as unsuitable for the matchups he had tested.

* * *

That evening, Leah opened the report while preparing her own practice schedule.

She stopped at a recording he had included almost as an afterthought. The situation was similar to one she had encountered at her last tournament.

She saved the recording and sent him a question about the original list.

The answer could wait until he finished his shift. In the meantime, she had a new scenario to reproduce.

Function: conclusión limitada → perspectiva independiente → conexión nueva → prueba pendiente. No anticipar éxito.
