# MSC-EX09 — convivencia, subtexto y espacio
Ocho entradas M73–M80 y tres bloques MB29–MB31. Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/DOMESTIC_SUBTEXT_EXCERPT_ANALYSIS.md. Ejercicios originales no canónicos.

## M73 — Identidad fuera de competición
Perfil público → encuentro cotidiano → diferencia específica.
Example: Adrian recognized the voice before he recognized the player. On the stream, he had never heard Marcus laugh that way.
Control: evitar estereotipos físicos y psicológicos.

## M74 — Deseo condicionado
Oferta atractiva → restricción privada → respuesta inesperada.
Example: Nora invited him to stay for the final games. He checked the time and asked whether she could send him the recordings instead.
Control: la restricción debe existir antes de la decisión.

## M75 — Revelación personal parcial
Pregunta razonable → verdad incompleta → límite preservado.
Example: “Are you coming on Sunday?” Adrian folded the schedule. “I have something at home to take care of.”
Control: distinguir lo que se oculta de lo que otros infieren.

## M76 — Desplazamiento temático
Pregunta sensible → respuesta suficiente → regreso a objetivo concreto.
Example: “It's been a difficult week,” Leah said. She opened the testing schedule. “Which of these dates can you still make?”
Control: no abusar de evasión como misterio artificial.

## M77 — Cuidado mediante logística
Necesidad comunicada → ajuste práctico → estado social.
Example: Marcus moved the session to the afternoon after Nora mentioned her evening shift.
Control: respetar consentimiento y costes de otros.

## M78 — Espacio explicado durante incorporación
Recorrido → objeto o distribución → necesidad institucional.
Example: The spare tables were folded against the wall. Leah explained that they cleared the room every Thursday for the beginner event.
Control: no inventar procedimiento real.

## M79 — Objetos como evidencia del pasado
Detalle presente → hipótesis sobre uso anterior → pregunta.
Example: The cabinet contained a row of labeled folders, except for one empty slot. Adrian wondered whether someone had taken a file home.
Control: una pista no demuestra una explicación.

## M80 — Reacción que abre un hilo
Pregunta ordinaria → cambio de conducta → explicación pendiente.
Example: When Adrian asked about the old team photograph, Nora took a moment before answering. “Ask me after the event.”
Control: registrar motivo futuro; no atribuir trauma sin evidencia.

## MB29 — Integración cotidiana
Adrian arrived before the testing session and found Marcus setting out plates.

“You're staying for dinner?”

“I've got work in an hour.”

Marcus checked the clock and handed him a container.

“Take some with you, then.”

Adrian had expected to spend the first evening proving he belonged at the table. Marcus seemed more concerned that he had eaten.

Function: incorporación, restricciones y cuidado práctico. No imponer deuda emocional.

## MB30 — Frontera personal
Leah asked why Adrian had missed the previous event.

“Family stuff.”

She waited, but he had already opened his notebook.

“Did anyone save the final?”

“I did,” she said, passing him her laptop.

There would be another chance to ask. For now, they had a match to review.

Function: respuesta parcial y respeto de límites; fijar POV coherente.

## MB31 — Un lugar con historia
The shop owner unlocked a room at the back. There were two tables and a cabinet full of binders.

Adrian noticed a framed team shirt above the cabinet. Someone had carefully removed the name tag beneath it.

“Who used this room before us?”

The owner set the keys on the table.

“People I used to play with.”

He began explaining the closing procedure.

Adrian left the question there.

Function: indicio ambiental y respuesta limitada. No establecer desaparición, conflicto ni identidad como hechos.

## Selector
M73–M77 para convivencia, subtexto y cuidado; M78–M80 para espacio, pistas y pasado. Registrar conocimiento y consecuencias, mantener voces independientes. Relacionados con M04/M07/M25/M35/M50/M51.
