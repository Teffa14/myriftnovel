# MSC-EX11 — reclutamiento y proyección de talento
Ejercicios originales en inglés, no canon. Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/RECRUITMENT_PRACTICE_OPTIMIZATION_ANALYSIS.md. Validar reglas, resultados y datos; no copiar texto fuente.

## M91 — Inferencia por señales convergentes
Estructura: Dos señales parciales → hipótesis común → comprobación.
Example: The team had seen the same unusual choice in two published lists. Nora wanted to find out whether the players had arrived at it independently.
Control: No atribuir coordinación sin evidencia.

## M92 — Analogía histórica disputada
Estructura: Precedente → interpretación A → interpretación B → decisión.
Example: Marcus saw a repeat of last season. Leah thought the circumstances were different and asked him to compare the actual results.
Control: No equiparar trayectorias.

## M93 — Potencial frente a rendimiento
Estructura: Habilidad específica observada → posibilidad futura → evaluación pendiente.
Example: The applicant had explained a difficult decision clearly. Nora still wanted to see how he handled a full practice match.
Control: No convertir potencial en ranking.

## M94 — Coste de esperar
Estructura: Oportunidad → incertidumbre → alternativa temporal → acción proporcional.
Example: The next testing weekend was nearly full. Leah offered the newcomer a trial session rather than a permanent place.
Control: No garantizar contratación.

## M95 — Indicio histórico interrumpido
Estructura: Referencia al pasado → límite conversacional → hilo abierto.
Example: Marcus mentioned the former captain. Nora checked the schedule and said they could discuss him another day.
Control: Registrar resolución; no fabricar trauma.

## M96 — Comunicación demorada por actividad propia
Estructura: Mensaje enviado → destinatario ocupado → consecuencia.
Example: Adrian saw the invitation after his shift. By then the group had already changed its practice time.
Control: No confundir demora con rechazo.

## M97 — Umbral de progresión cumplido
Estructura: Requisito sembrado → logro → acceso nuevo.
Example: Adrian finally had the recordings the group had requested. He sent them before asking for another trial.
Control: Verificar requisito; acceso no garantiza éxito.

## M98 — Acceso desigual tras hito compartido
Estructura: Mismo logro → recursos distintos → caminos divergentes.
Example: Both players qualified for the event. Only one could afford to book the trip that evening.
Control: Separar mérito y recursos.

## MB36 — Scouting con evidencia limitada
Leah paused the recording at the decision that had caught her attention.

“He's worth asking to practice with us.”

Marcus looked at the rest of the match. The player had lost.

“Based on that one turn?”

“Based on the question he asked afterward. I want to see how he tests the answer.”

They sent an invitation for the next session.

Function: Señal, límite y prueba de talento.

## MB37 — Precedente discutido
Marcus remembered the last time a new list had appeared at their local events. By the following month, the group had spent most of its practice time trying to catch up.

Nora opened the new results.

“This might be different.”

“Then let's find out before we change everything.”

They allocated one session to the unfamiliar list.

Function: Memoria institucional, incertidumbre y decisión proporcionada.

## MB38 — Desfase de expectativas
The team sent Adrian a message just before his shift began. Leah wanted to discuss his testing notes; Marcus wanted to know whether he could join them on Saturday.

Adrian read the message after closing the store.

He had already promised to cover another shift.

He sent back two dates he could manage instead.

Function: Agencia independiente, oportunidad y restricción.
