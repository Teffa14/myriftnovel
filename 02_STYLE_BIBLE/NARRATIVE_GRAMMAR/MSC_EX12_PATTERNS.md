# MSC-EX12 — práctica y especialización
Ejercicios originales en inglés, no canon. Fuente: ../../01_RESEARCH/REFERENCE_LIBRARY/works/MSC/RECRUITMENT_PRACTICE_OPTIMIZATION_ANALYSIS.md. Validar reglas, resultados y datos; no copiar texto fuente.

## M99 — Acceso facilitado por otro
Estructura: Recurso faltante → apoyo explícito → responsabilidad.
Example: Nora lent Adrian a deck for the weekend and asked him to return it after practice.
Control: Distinguir préstamo y regalo.

## M100 — Error de ejecución situado
Estructura: Objetivo → error observable → consecuencia → corrección.
Example: Adrian missed the relevant detail in the practice position. Marcus marked the moment for their review.
Control: Validar jugada concreta con replay.

## M101 — Ayuda que conserva agencia
Estructura: Problema → apoyo permitido → aprendiz actúa.
Example: Leah showed him where to find the practice records. Adrian chose which game to review first.
Control: No asesorar durante torneo prohibido.

## M102 — Transferencia de observación a práctica
Estructura: Modelo visto → situación propia → ejecución → evaluación.
Example: Adrian had watched Nora approach the matchup on Tuesday. In his next practice session, he tried to explain the same decision in his own words.
Control: Comprensión no implica ejecución legal.

## M103 — Aprobación ligada a acción
Estructura: Acción verificable → respuesta específica → continuidad.
Example: Marcus nodded when Adrian identified the mistake in the notes. They moved on to the next recording.
Control: Evitar asombro desproporcionado.

## M104 — Fricción repetitiva comprimida
Estructura: Dificultad recurrente → patrón resumido → punto de cambio.
Example: The same scheduling problem affected three sessions. Nora moved their shared review to a different evening.
Control: No inventar resultados.

## M105 — Taxonomía por experiencia acumulada
Estructura: Varias observaciones → categorías → límites.
Example: After testing the two lists, Adrian separated problems that came from the opening from those that appeared much later.
Control: No asumir exhaustividad.

## M106 — Especialización con legado
Estructura: Contribución histórica → enfoque distintivo → problema actual.
Example: Leah had helped popularize a particular approach at the local club. Now she was testing whether it still worked against the newer lists.
Control: Reputación requiere evidencia y límites.

## MB39 — De ver a hacer
Adrian had watched Marcus review the matchup twice. This evening he sat on the other side of the table.

His first explanation was incomplete. When they checked the recording, he found a decision he had overlooked.

He wrote down the information he had actually possessed at that point.

They returned to the practice position and began again.

Function: Observación, error y práctica verificable.

## MB40 — Apoyo material
Nora set a deck box on the table.

“Use this until Sunday. I have another copy of the list.”

Adrian asked when she needed it back.

“Monday morning.”

He added the date to his calendar. For the first time that week, he could spend the evening testing instead of looking for cards.

Function: Préstamo con límite y cambio de oportunidad.

## MB41 — Identidad competitiva histórica
Marcus had learned the matchup from Leah's old recordings. Her approach had once been unusual at the shop; now several regulars used variations of it.

When he asked whether she was bringing the same list next month, she shook her head.

“People have had a year to prepare for it.”

She handed him the latest testing notes.

Function: Legado, adaptación y continuidad.
