## M107 — Comparación con ejemplar experto
Estructura: Artefacto conocido → diferencia observable → hipótesis.
Example: Nora compared the two testing sheets. The more useful one separated starting conditions from outcomes.
Control: No inferir causalidad universal.

## M108 — Reinterpretación de una instrucción
Estructura: Consejo previo → aplicación errónea → evidencia → nueva lectura.
Example: Adrian had interpreted consistency as playing the same opening every time. The notes suggested he needed a plan for several different ones.
Control: Precisar concepto.

## M109 — Mejora versionada
Estructura: Versión anterior → modificación → resultado parcial → límite.
Example: He saved both lists and recorded which change he had made before the next practice game.
Control: No atribuir resultado a múltiples cambios sin control.

## M110 — Magnitud relativa y absoluta
Estructura: Métrica inicial → nueva métrica → interpretación contextual.
Example: The revised schedule gave them two additional games per session. Nora still wanted to know whether the extra games addressed useful questions.
Control: No equiparar mejora de proxy con victoria.

## M111 — Señal confundida con ruido
Estructura: Aviso repetido → explicación habitual → comprobación → sorpresa.
Example: Adrian assumed the notification came from the group calendar. When he opened it, he found a personal invitation.
Control: Evitar ignorancia artificial.

## M112 — Convergencia de objetivos distintos
Estructura: A quiere X → B quiere Y → encuentro → aclaración pendiente.
Example: Adrian arrived to discuss travel costs. Leah had invited him because she wanted to understand his last deck change.
Control: No transferir motivos privados.

## M113 — Jerarquía revelada por conducta
Estructura: Observador desconoce autoridad → interacción de otros → identificación.
Example: The organizer handed the question to someone Adrian had not met. She introduced herself as the person responsible for the event schedule.
Control: No deducir autoridad solo por estética.

## M114 — Cierre en identificación significativa
Estructura: Encuentro ambiguo → revelación de identidad → nueva pregunta.
Example: Adrian recognized the name as soon as the visitor introduced herself. He had been studying her published matches all week.
Control: La reacción futura no es resultado.

## MB42 — Corregir una interpretación
Adrian opened Nora's old practice records beside his own. Both contained the result of each game. Hers also indicated the position they had intended to study.

He had spent the week tracking wins and losses without preserving the question behind each session.

He copied the headings into a new file, then went back through the recordings to fill in what he could verify.

Several entries remained blank.

Function: Comparación, revisión de método y límites.

## MB43 — Mejora que requiere prueba
The new spreadsheet took less time to complete. Adrian showed Nora the two versions.

“Can you still reconstruct the decisions from this one?” she asked.

He tried with the last recording. One detail was missing.

He restored the column and saved the revision.

The faster method would only be useful if it preserved the information they needed.

Function: Optimización, tradeoff y prueba parcial.

## MB44 — Reunión con agendas distintas
Adrian had prepared a short list of questions about the team's travel arrangements.

When he reached the shop, Leah was waiting with two other players. One of them had brought a printout of his practice notes.

“We wanted to ask you about this,” she said.

Adrian put his own notebook on the table.

He had expected to discuss Saturday. Before doing that, he would have to explain a decision he had made the previous week.

Function: Convergencia, expectativa divergente y apertura de negociación.
