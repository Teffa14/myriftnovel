## M115 — Compromisos separables
Estructura: Dos ofertas → evaluar por separado → resolver una → mantener otra abierta.
Example: Adrian agreed to review the recordings, but asked for the season schedule before committing to the team.
Control: Comprobar separabilidad y estados.

## M116 — Revaloración retrospectiva
Estructura: Nueva oferta → comparar decisión anterior → ajustar expectativa.
Example: The new offer made Leah reconsider the terms she had accepted last season.
Control: No inferir precio universal.

## M117 — Presión social frente a autonomía
Estructura: Expectativa ajena → límite propio → respuesta proporcional.
Example: Marcus expected an answer that evening. Nora asked for the written terms before making a decision.
Control: No glorificar presión.

## M118 — Oferta con incentivos múltiples
Estructura: Propuesta → componentes → restricciones → evaluación.
Example: The stipend covered practice, but Adrian still needed to understand the travel requirements.
Control: Separar pago, condiciones y promesas.

## M119 — Transferencia con coste irreversible
Estructura: Acuerdo → cesión → facultad perdida → oportunidad restante.
Example: After transferring the collection, Adrian could no longer use those copies in his list.
Control: Verificar propiedad y términos.

## M120 — Trámite como causalidad
Estructura: Acuerdo verbal → formalización → estado comprobado.
Example: Nora waited for the signed confirmation before updating the team's budget.
Control: No confundir etapas.

## M121 — Confirmación material de recompensa
Estructura: Expectativa → evidencia de recepción → estado actualizado.
Example: The payment arrived on Friday. Adrian entered it in the account before booking his train.
Control: Separar bruto, neto y saldo.

## M122 — Límite físico tras logro
Estructura: Objetivo cumplido → señal corporal → decisión de descanso.
Example: Adrian closed the laptop when he realized he was rereading the same line.
Control: No diagnosticar ni romantizar agotamiento.

## MB45 — Dos decisiones independientes
Nora slid two documents across the table. One described the work they needed before Saturday. The other covered the next three months.

Adrian read the shorter one first.

“I can finish this by Friday. For the rest, I need to know how often you expect me to travel.”

Nora separated the documents and wrote down the dates they still needed to discuss.

Function: Resolver una propuesta y mantener otra abierta; sin contrato confirmado.

## MB46 — Resultado y compromiso
The buyer confirmed the transfer while Adrian was still packing his binder.

He checked the amount before marking the sale as paid. The cards were no longer his, and he removed them from the inventory he had been using for practice.

He would have to borrow replacements if he wanted to test the old list again.

Function: Pago y pérdida de propiedad con consecuencias.

## MB47 — Límite y cierre
Nora had finished the last review when she noticed the time. She had promised to be home an hour earlier.

She saved the notes and sent the team a short message.

The remaining recordings could wait until tomorrow.

Function: Cierre local y límite cotidiano, no promesa de rendimiento.
