## M123 — Revelación de identidad retrospectiva
Estructura: Acción anónima anterior → identidad nueva → reinterpretación.
Example: The organizer recognized the account name on the registration request. It belonged to someone she had worked with before.
Control: No suponer conocimiento ajeno.

## M124 — Segmentación por incentivos
Estructura: Actores distintos → motivaciones → hipótesis de búsqueda.
Example: Leah separated players selling spare copies from collectors looking for specific printings.
Control: Evitar estereotipos y verificar datos.

## M125 — Búsqueda dirigida por hipótesis
Estructura: Información disponible → fuente probable → verificación.
Example: Nora checked the event's published records before asking the organizer about the missing result.
Control: Fuentes públicas o autorizadas.

## M126 — Experiencia pasada como modelo operativo
Estructura: Error previo → regla provisional → decisión actual.
Example: Marcus remembered how often he had confused a popular list with a successful one. This time he checked both numbers.
Control: No convertir precedente en certeza.

## M127 — Intentos fallidos comprimidos
Estructura: Búsqueda repetida → resultados negativos → hallazgo específico.
Example: The first four listings had expired. The fifth seller confirmed that the card was still available.
Control: No inventar disponibilidad.

## M128 — Precio de reserva subjetivo
Estructura: Oferta → rechazo → revisión → aceptación voluntaria o salida.
Example: The seller declined Adrian's offer. He asked whether a trade would be more useful to her.
Control: Respetar consentimiento, no garantizar venta.

## M129 — Confirmación mediante fuente independiente
Estructura: Hipótesis → testimonio/documento → información nueva.
Example: Leah checked the published team roster before updating her notes about the event.
Control: Verificar autoridad y alcance de la fuente.

## M130 — Payoff de hilo secundario
Estructura: Detalle sembrado → evidencia posterior → consecuencia concreta.
Example: The contact Nora had saved months earlier finally became relevant when the team needed a practice venue.
Control: Registrar apertura, prueba y resolución.

## MB48 — Experiencia aplicada a búsqueda
Marcus had spent most of the morning checking listings for the missing cards.

Leah asked whether he had looked at the store's trade board.

“Those people usually want exchanges, not cash.”

“Exactly. We have something they might want.”

They checked the posted requests before contacting anyone.

Function: Segmentación de incentivos y búsqueda legítima; sin trato confirmado.

## MB49 — Resultado de búsqueda limitado
Nora reviewed the public results from the last three events. Two lists were available, and one player had published only a brief report.

She added the missing information to the testing brief instead of filling it in from memory.

They had enough to prepare for two matchups. The third would require a different approach.

Function: Fuentes, incertidumbre y decisión proporcional.

## MB50 — Recuperación de un hilo
Adrian recognized the name on the volunteer schedule. He had met the player at the first local event, when they had discussed finding somewhere quiet to practice.

He sent a message asking whether the offer was still open.

The reply came later that afternoon. The room was available on Tuesdays.

Function: Detalle anterior, comunicación y resolución concreta; verificar permiso.
