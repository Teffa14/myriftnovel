## M131 — Precio experimental con exposición limitada
Level: L3. Evidence: EX16 B2–B3 in source analysis.
Structure: publicar una unidad → fijar condiciones verificables → asumir coste/tiempo → esperar respuesta → revisar precio.
Conditions: Mercado legítimo, coste tolerable, reglas transparentes.
Expected effect: expectativa basada en incertidumbre cuantificable (editorial hypothesis).
Example: Adrian listed a single spare copy before deciding whether to sell the rest.
Failure/control: Una venta no determina demanda total; no ocultar gastos ni manipular pujas.
Related IDs: M17/M60/M90.

## M132 — Asignación de recompensa entre reservas
Level: L3. Evidence: EX17 B1 in source analysis.
Structure: ingreso realizado → obligaciones y metas → separar reservas → nueva posibilidad.
Conditions: Fondos recibidos, categorías distintas.
Expected effect: mostrar avance sin gastar el premio automáticamente (editorial hypothesis).
Example: Nora moved the prize money into her travel account before opening the next registration page.
Failure/control: No asignar dinero pendiente ni contabilizar dos veces.
Related IDs: M42/M68/M121.

## M133 — Reputación que se propaga fuera del POV
Level: L3/L6. Evidence: EX17 B3 in source analysis.
Structure: resultado observable → publicación → voces independientes → reacción institucional → protagonista aún ajeno.
Conditions: Medio y audiencia establecidos; resultado real.
Expected effect: crear consecuencias sociales y asimetría (editorial hypothesis).
Example: While Adrian was at work, the local club reposted the standings and two organizers asked about his list.
Failure/control: No inventar cifras de alcance o convertir fama en habilidad.

## M135 — Certeza condicional frente a resultado
Level: L2/L3. Evidence: EX18 B3 in source analysis.
Structure: métrica alta → generalización del aprendiz → objeción experta → condiciones pendientes.
Conditions: Distinguir efecto, activación, objetivo y respuesta.
Expected effect: anticipar explicación sin inventar desenlace (editorial hypothesis).
Example: Even if the opening was guaranteed, Nora asked, would it still leave them a favorable position?
Failure/control: No sustituir legalidad, timing o replay por probabilidad; comprobar denominadores.

## M136 — Método experto revelado mediante artefactos
Level: L2. Evidence: EX18 B4 in source analysis.
Structure: espacio u objeto → organización observable → inferencia limitada sobre práctica.
Conditions: Artefactos con relación funcional a actividad.
Expected effect: caracterizar mediante evidencia material (editorial hypothesis).
Example: The folders were labeled by matchup and date; Adrian realized the team had kept records of its earlier experiments.
Failure/control: Una colección ordenada no prueba habilidad ni resultados.
Related IDs: M79/M107.

## MB51 — A price is not a budget
Patterns: M131, M132, M68.

Adrian had three spare copies in his binder. He listed one of them and recorded the fees he would have to pay if it sold.

Two days later a buyer made an offer. Before replying, Adrian checked how much of the travel bill he could actually cover after the sale.

The other copies stayed in the binder. One buyer was not enough to tell him whether he could sell the rest at the same price.

Function: price discovery, allocation, uncertain demand. Noncanon; hypothetical, no validated game results.

She marked the page and sent the sponsor a question.

The standings would still be there when she finished.

Function: public/private split and unresolved nonfinancial term. Noncanon; hypothetical, no validated game results.

## MB53 — From a claim to a test
Patterns: M135, M136, M108.

Adrian pointed to the frequency column.

“So we can rely on that opening?”

Nora pulled up three recordings. All three had begun the same way. Only one had ended in a win.

“What did the other player do differently?”

Adrian opened his notebook. He had been recording the opening, but not the decisions that followed it.

They started with the first recording.

Function: distinguish frequency from outcomes; artifacts support review. Noncanon; hypothetical, no validated game results.
