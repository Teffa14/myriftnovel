# CH0009 — abstract narrative variants

## TLDV027
A previously accepted mundane responsibility conflicts with a fleeting opportunity. The affected person refuses displacement and the other honors the commitment despite an actual opportunity cost. Establish legitimate competing goals and preserve the consequences in later continuity.

## TLDV028
Recurring logistical absence is misread as a social preference. Direct clarification enables an independent, reciprocal collaborative commitment. Preserve both participants' agency; avoid copying the source scene, dialogue or payoff sequence.

These are abstract mechanisms only. Distinctive source realizations are retired in the private archive; no manuscript excerpts or prospective plot details are included.
