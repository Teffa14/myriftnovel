## TLDV033
An independently justified workplace request creates a voluntary material gain at the cost of recovery and a personal opportunity. Show actual work, viable refusal, the authority figure's objective, recorded time, and downstream effects. Distinguish projected compensation from received funds.

## TLDV034
A previously diagnosed strategic weakness survives an attempted fix because new options still draw on the original constrained resource. Use independent adversarial choices, stable test versions, bounded observations, reciprocal credit and an actionable next hypothesis; do not equate exploratory results with causal proof.
