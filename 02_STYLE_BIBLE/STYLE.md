Internal thoughts naturally integrated; italic direct thought sparingly. Prose concrete, idiomatic and sensory-selective; vary syntax, avoid generic motivational narration, repetitive contrast constructions and translation artifacts. No copy of reference expression, archetypal names or scenes.
 
Default chapter 1,700–2,200 words, extend when the scene earns it, never pad. Start in action, tension or relationship; anchor place and immediate goal quickly. Every scene has a change in state and a causal link; quiet moments can alter trust, perception or commitment. Close with genuine consequence, question, decision or emotional payoff; avoid automatic cliffhanger every chapter.
 
Matches: show stakes before technical detail; give novice reader the decision and consequence, expert reader enough precise state to assess it. Explain a rule when it changes choices. ## AD-0009 — Narrative time, not an operational log
The private production ledger must retain accurate story dates, times or ranges, timezones, durations, ordering and uncertainties. Do not reproduce this record in the manuscript. Write the passage of time through observable activity, causal transitions, changing surroundings, arrivals, interruptions, dialogue and ordinary temporal language when useful. Do not replace each timestamp with an equally repetitive vague transition; often adjacent actions need no temporal marker at all.
Use an explicit clock time, date or precise elapsed duration only when the reader benefits from the precision: a meaningful deadline or appointment, a competitive clock, a consequential delay or timing-dependent choice, an otherwise ambiguous scene transition, or a deliberate emotional effect. Establish the necessary anchor once and repeat it only when the repetition serves the scene. Where a character's attention to the clock is motivated, preserve that viewpoint without mechanically reporting each check. Never conceal information needed to understand fairness, causality or game mechanics.
Editorial diagnostic: inspect numeric and spelled-out clock times, exact elapsed intervals, repeated clock checks and paragraph-opening time markers in context. For each unnecessary marker, delete or integrate it into the surrounding action rather than mechanically substituting synonyms. Do not impose a numerical quota, prohibit chapter-opening timestamps categorically, or alter established chronology to improve rhythm. Approved prose remains unchanged; apply to new drafts and revisions.
