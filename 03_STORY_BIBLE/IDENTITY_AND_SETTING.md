Title: The Final Draw (author's choice). Working subtitle: A Competitive TCG Novel; for storefront search, test an original subtitle and check title/trademark clearance (Ridley Pearson homonym from 2025). Original cover without Riot logos or art. Language: idiomatic American English. Genre: contemporary sports fiction / competitive gaming / coming-of-age adult crossover. Audience hypothesis: TCG players first, readers of sports fiction and character-driven serials second. No LitRPG tag, magic, isekai or omniscient superpower.
 
Location locked: Los Angeles metro area, California, USA. Fictional game store, teams, businesses, players, organizers and fictional tournament results; real geographic context and public game rules may be referenced accurately. Start Saturday 2026-11-28, after Radiance banlist effective 2026-11-23; historical snapshot dated 2026-11-25. Avoid invented real-world schedules or asserting unverified 2027 formats. Future competition types must be checked before drafts. Dates of fictional local unsanctioned events may be defined freely, but distinguish them from actual sanctioned programs.
 
One-line hook: He can read a game better than he can read people.
Pitch: Nico Vega can spot a mistake in a tournament recording before most players know what they are looking at. He cannot afford the cards, the entry fees, or another mistake at work. When a losing match earns him an invitation to a struggling practice group, he discovers that building a competitive deck is easier than building a team that trusts him. As the game changes and the cost of competing rises, the decisions that take him closer to the top threaten the life he is trying to build.
 
The fiction foregrounds people, real strategic decision-making and consequences. Rules and card data are separately versioned; no claims of affiliation. Primary internal draft uses Riftbound; no commercial release until rights review and any necessary written license; contingency uses an independently designed original TCG, not a renamed clone.
