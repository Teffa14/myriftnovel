# Antagonist architecture — AD-0015

Author direction: create recurring irritating, competitive antagonisms with satisfying earned defeats over time. Use the [community conflict database](../01_RESEARCH/COMMUNITY/CONFLICT_CASES_2026-11-27.json) for twelve conflict mechanisms (JC01–12) and eight fictional long-range candidate dossiers (ANT01–08). Exact identities, names, chapter ranges, match results and relationships remain prospective; establish and approve privately before incorporation. Do not equate existing named rivals with any reported misconduct.

## Build earned resentment
Introduce a credible competitor pursuing a real independent goal. Establish the person's competence through supported competition or concrete action; demonstrate the specific objectionable behavior through scenes, not narrator labels. Give the initial target a meaningful reason to be affected: a scarce opportunity, social belonging, pride, earned result or trust. Let the antagonist succeed sometimes, be respected by others for intelligible reasons, and have consequences that persist after a match. Differentiate interpersonal contempt, exclusion, public reputation, unreliable cooperation and tournament pressure. Do not give all antagonists the same attitude, speech pattern, deck or fate. Allow principled rivalry, correct rulings and ordinary friendly competition as counterweights.

## Payoff planning
For each selected candidate maintain private evidence-linked fields: ANT/JC IDs, fictional character ID, independent objective, demonstrated competence, first incident, consequences for multiple affected people, later encounter, changed preparation or relationships, credible reversal, planned payoff, verified result or alternative, aftermath, actual approved source locations and status. Avoid guaranteed victory in a particular match; validate competitive scenes under G3-scene/L2 where applicable. Do not force a dishonest loss, invented penalty, caricature, mass cheering, gloating or public humiliation as a resolution. A competitive defeat may satisfy readers because a previously vulnerable character has earned the ability to compete and assert boundaries. Conduct complaints go to impartial officials; ordinary judge calls and legal strict play are legitimate. Do not imply proof of intent based on frustration; never teach cheating, exploiting time, manipulating adjudication or retaliation.

## Serial integration
Review the active rival ledger at each 20-chapter arc close and the AD-0011 movement checkpoints. Maintain variation across local, regional and higher-level competition, interpersonal and institutional conflicts. Build and reinforce some relationships across multiple volumes; use interim setbacks and partial rewards without delaying all catharsis. No fixed number of adversaries per chapter, mandatory loss or abusive behavior quota. Preserve AD-0008 joy, AD-0010/0012 emotional architecture, LPM 1.0, character independence, Chapter 500 ending, prior directives and approved prose. Only author-approved narrative developments become canon. Record observed reader feedback separately from editorial expectations.
