## Character-specific requirements

### CD-N — Nico Vega
Established baseline: night-shift logistics, paused community-college studies, shared household with Lucia, ambition to afford competitive travel without loans; domestic responsibilities, extra work and fatigue already create constraints. Basketball, Argentine rock and emerging social ties offer non-game avenues.
- CD-N01: Accumulated professional frustration. Show actual work, authority, coworkers, unfair or conflicting expectations where justified, competence, fatigue and a material consequence; the workplace must recur as a real setting, not a reported schedule.
- CD-N02: Rent and financial pressure. Track actual household obligations, income, transport, food, necessary costs and game-fund boundaries. Show a choice that changes what he can afford or do.
- CD-N03: Deferred aspirations. Develop what interrupted education and limited career prospects cost him, including concrete alternatives or opportunities and the emotional consequences of choosing.
- CD-N04: Social and economic inequality. Give him social opportunities outside the game where time or money creates a credible conflict, and allow friendship, embarrassment, humor, pride and reciprocity.
- CD-N05: Deeper motivation to change his life. Connect competitive ambition to independent dignity, relationships and a specific desired future. Show vulnerability, decisions and subsequent behavior, not an abstract monologue.

### CD-J — June Park
Established baseline: freelance design work, independent qualification goal, architectural photography, fashion, accessories and visual design, off-duty humor and a developing friendship with Maya. Historical assessment found little romantic tension with Nico.
- CD-J01: Difficult clients, deadlines, scope disputes or competing professional demands. Make her decisions affect pay, reputation, personal time or preparation.
- CD-J02: Financial insecurity and professional ambition. Establish realistic income uncertainty and commitments through approved facts; show consequences without inventing a current balance.
- CD-J03: Independent social life, including nightlife when appropriate, friendships, hobbies and choices outside competition. Develop recognizable ABG-inspired style in accordance with AD-0004 and existing visual identity; no ethnicity-based personality assumptions.
- CD-J04: Romantic experiences, reciprocal attraction, emotional and adult sensual chemistry where appropriate; develop her ability to initiate, decline, disagree and change her mind according to the private trajectory.
- CD-J05: Vulnerability and personal stakes. Reveal private pressures, fear of failure or uncertainty through a consequential choice, preserving independent career and competitive competence.

## Cross-character development gates
CD-G01: Every major character needs goals, choices, stakes and relationships that would still matter without the game. Each on-page personal conflict must alter a concrete state (money, work, family, studies, health, reputation, access, plans or relationship).
CD-G02: Develop the six life domains in parallel across the serial: competitive, economic, professional/educational, social, familial and sentimental. Track all ten historical diagnostic dimensions at arc reviews; distinguish character appeal and chemistry from actual romantic progression. Never force every dimension into each chapter.
CD-G03: Sustain interpersonal connections and emotional vulnerability through distinct voices, meaningful actions, reciprocity, disagreement, repair and consequences. Adult romance and sexual tension develop through context-appropriate mutual choices. Serious losses require preparation and lasting aftermath consistent with private directives. No prescribed tragedy for each character.
CD-G04: Preserve differentiated hobbies and everyday enjoyment; use them to create friendships, opportunities, conflicts and relief rather than treating them as character development by themselves.
CD-G05: Every major character must have an independently meaningful non-game thread. Before each new arc, identify active requirements and a planned progression for each principal character relevant to the arc. Carry unresolved gaps forward with owner, dependency and earliest plausible opportunity. A character absent from an arc requires a reason and a later review point, not a contrived cameo.
CD-G06: In each chapter brief record relevant CD IDs (or reasoned N/A), existing evidence, concrete immediate objective, opposition, competing options, chosen action, cost, state change, open consequence and next dependency. In editorial review locate actual textual evidence; mark planned, setup, progressing, delivered, aftermath or deferred with reason. A mention, hobby, promise or draft outline alone cannot count as delivery. Use rolling three/four-chapter review consistent with LIFE_PRESSURE and SERIAL_QUALITY_PROTOCOL; at every 20-chapter arc close review all six characters and ten dimensions. Missing justified coverage is an editorial issue to resolve or defer with a specific plan; no fixed per-chapter POV quotas.
CD-G07: Maintain a private character-development ledger with character ID, requirement ID, chapter/scene locator, approved version, prior/new state, observed consequences, status, planned next beat and dependency. Update occurred state only after exact author approval. Preserve the original CH11 score baseline; subsequent subjective ratings require actual manuscript evidence and a separately dated review. No numerical claims of improvement merely because requirements were added.
