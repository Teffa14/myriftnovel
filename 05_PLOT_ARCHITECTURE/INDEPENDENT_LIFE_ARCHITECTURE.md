# LIFE IS HAPPENING — Independent Life Architecture (AD-0016)

Author direction, 2026-11-27. Characters have independent human lives across the 500-main-chapter story. Their education, work, family, friendships, intimacy, creative interests, trips, celebrations, wounds, reunions and changes of direction can carry complete main chapters and linked sequences. Maintain intrinsic pleasure and quiet chapters under AD-0008; do not make every ordinary scene a crisis, competition lesson, or setup for future tragedy. Distinguish ordinary lived texture from sustained independent life trajectories. No new specific future event or biography is established as canon by this standard.

## Twelve transferable standards

LIH01 Independent vocation — show ongoing non-TCG work and its own meaningful milestones, stakeholders and choices; careers are not merely tournament funding.
LIH02 Home across time — accumulate ordinary domestic history and show changing relationships, responsibilities and living arrangements without turning each scene into an account ledger.
LIH03 Persistent friendship — independent circles, invitations, pleasures, friction and reciprocity; people have relationships beyond the protagonist and may progress off page.
LIH04 Return and changed self — establish a prior connection, plausible elapsed life and a present reason for reunion; reveal change through action and perspective rather than exposition.
LIH05 Calendar and anticipation — give readers personally meaningful occasions to anticipate; follow through on some plans exactly as expected. Avoid repeated countdowns and compulsory disruption.
LIH06 Independent intimacy — affection, attraction, boundaries, mismatched priorities and changing needs; preserve established author-directed romance and consent, no tournament-win romance reward.
LIH07 Bodies and time — age, health, energy, recovery and physical identity have ordinary consequences; no invented medical claims, gratuitous trauma or additional binding losses.
LIH08 Place as social history — recurring locations and social worlds beyond hobby stores; people have different relationships to familiar places. Verify consequential real-world facts.
LIH09 Autonomy and family — family members pursue their own futures and can change established obligations; no reduction to crises or rescue.
LIH10 Creative community — show independent collaborators, creative work, ownership, standards and earned satisfaction; avoid reskinning the repetitive competitive-testing structure.
LIH11 Travel and transition — trips with their own reasons, realistic feasibility, discoveries and enjoyment; not just transportation to events.
LIH12 Interior contradiction — develop particular hopes, pride, discomfort, ambivalence and self-deception through lived perspective. Avoid forced aphorisms, explanatory monologues and artificial eloquence.

These are original functional abstractions developed from the author's request and a preceding synopsis-level comparison of slice-of-life, drama and coming-of-age works. No full source-corpus analysis, linguistic measurements or claim of copying a work's voice. The project house style LPM and NDM remain applicable.

## Introduction and deployment
Use an established preference, relationship, occupation, routine or independently motivated present request as the entry. Example mechanisms: an invitation needed today; an outside collaborator asking for a small task; an existing appointment that causes an absence; a returning acquaintance with a visible change; an ordinary object being used for its present purpose; an anticipated occasion and its fulfillment. Introduce only information needed for the scene. New biographical facts require actual continuity review. Track off-page activity privately; reveal changes through perspective-compatible evidence. No hidden future fact presented as already occurred.

Before selecting a life thread, ask whether it would still make sense if the protagonist stopped competing. Let interests and ambitions overlap naturally with the game but do not turn all of them into competitive tools. A quiet episode can reward readers through humor, affection, pleasure, recognition or insight. A sustained thread needs a distinct goal, some on-page realization, and credible follow-through. Compress repeated preparation when it offers no additional experience. A chapter may be wholly outside the game.
