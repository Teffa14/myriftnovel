# Slice of life — AD-0008
Binding author direction, 2026-11-26. Make everyday life and life through Riftbound recurring, intrinsically rewarding narrative material in The Last Draw. The reader should enjoy spending time with the cast, feel part of the local community, and accumulate authentic memories alongside competitive progress. Apply in main chapters as well as optional supplements; entire main chapters may center on ordinary or social experiences without a competitive match. This is prospective planning, not a change to any approved scene.

## Four complementary categories

### SL-E — Everyday Life
SL-E01 — Home and routines: cooking together, sharing meals, groceries, cleaning, laundry, commuting, errands, lazy Sundays, small household mishaps and ordinary rest.
SL-E02 — Individual interests: basketball, music, photography, dancing, crafts, film nights, clothing, personal projects and discovering new interests, consistent with established preferences.
SL-E03 — Work and study as lived environments: ordinary conversations, small successes, teasing among coworkers, lunch breaks, study sessions and relief after a long day. Do not require every work scene to be a crisis.
SL-E04 — Occasions and breaks: birthdays, holidays, day trips, vacations, celebrations and small rituals; verify calendar, geography, access and affordability when concrete.
SL-E05 — Unremarkable intimacy: sitting together, spontaneous messages, sharing a snack, enjoying silence, late-night conversation and the familiarity of returning places.

### SL-T — TCG Lifestyle
SL-T01 — Collecting: binders, preferred art, foils, sentimental cards, deck boxes, sleeves, collection organization, displaying a prized pull and different collecting goals.
SL-T02 — Opening products: friends opening boosters, anticipation, surprise, disappointing pulls and celebrations; use plausible purchases and authentic dated product contents, not invented odds or guaranteed value.
SL-T03 — Trading and shopping: browsing singles, negotiating fair trades, buying or selling cards, lending and returning cards with consent, budget differences and conversations with staff.
SL-T04 — Play for pleasure: odd decks, favorite champions, casual matches, teaching a beginner, experiments without a competitive objective and funny situations arising from lawful play. Do not turn every game into training, a lesson or a technical lecture.
SL-T05 — Local culture: waiting for pairings, food after locals, shop routines, spectators, regulars, collectors, new players, recurring in-jokes and conversations unrelated to the current tournament.
SL-T06 — Community discourse: group chats, memes, card art, banlist opinions, playful complaints, speculative brews and different play styles; distinguish character opinion from rules or verified facts. Generate original speech and use no identifiable real player's distinctive anecdotes.
SL-T07 — Shared outings: rides to stores, conventions, travel with friends, browsing vendors, meals and downtime between rounds; travel may be enjoyable in its own right. Establish practical cost and scheduling.

### SL-S — Social Life
SL-S01 — Reciprocal friendship: voluntary invitations, ordinary favors, shared laughter, celebrating small personal successes, and memorable rituals. Characters can disagree, reconcile or simply enjoy each other's company.
SL-S02 — Independent social circles: friends, classmates, colleagues, local regulars and gatherings that would exist without Nico. Maintain continuity for recurring characters and meaningful independent activities.
SL-S03 — Going out: markets, cafés, food, sports, music, parties, nightlife and preparing for a social occasion, in line with individual tastes and established finances. Keep cultural identity and personal choices distinct.
SL-S04 — Adult attraction and dating: flirting, humor, clothing, ordinary affection, awkward moments, conversations and mutual choices that develop real relationships. Follow AD-0004, AD-0006 and its amendment; no predetermined new pairings or public spoilers.
SL-S05 — Shared milestones: birthdays, career or study achievements, tournament celebrations, new experiences, and the pleasure of having people with whom to share them. Establish rituals organically, without making every joyful occasion ominous.

### SL-C — Comfort Chapters
SL-C01 — A primarily relaxed main chapter or interlude built around a recognizable ordinary occasion, not obligatory crisis, match, dramatic revelation or cliffhanger.
SL-C02 — Emotional contrast: give characters and readers breathing room after intense arcs; show pleasure, humor, genuine affection and recovery without requiring future tragedy to justify a scene.
SL-C03 — Small narrative movement: an invitation accepted, familiarity deepened, taste discovered, ordinary task accomplished, shared memory or changed perception may be sufficient. Do not manufacture stakes or force a subplot resolution to justify every beat.
SL-C04 — Distinct experiences: vary location, group configuration, activities, voices and emotional register. Avoid recycling an earlier outing, joke, food ritual, romantic gesture or scene architecture as a formula.
