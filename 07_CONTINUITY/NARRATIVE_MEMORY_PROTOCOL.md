# Mandatory narrative continuity memory v1.0 — 2026-11-25
This protocol augments CANON.md and NDM 2.0; it does not replace either. It applies to all chapters, supplements, revisions and optional interludes. No new manuscript may be approved with unresolved material continuity contradictions.

## Private source of truth
Retrieve the approved archive and latest private continuity sidecar from ChatGPT Library /The Last Draw - Private Archive/. Initial sidecar: TLD_continuity_private_v1.zip (78 source-linked records, 8 timeline entries; 25 approved and 53 provisional records). Keep full private records, source text, chronology details, character knowledge, and spoilers outside GitHub. Verify hashes against the approved archive. Sidecar changes must be versioned and reconciled with the authoritative archive; do not overwrite approved text or promote an unapproved draft. If a source is inaccessible, report the blocked scope rather than invent history. Before approval resolve source-version conflicts and validate exact revised bytes.

## Required record types
Use stable entity and event IDs. Maintain chronology (story date/time/range/timezone, before/after constraints, location, participants, POV and certainty); immutable facts and time-dependent state; physical attributes and wardrobe; injuries and healing; objects, money, cards and ownership; home/work/study schedules; personal interests, habits and aspirations; relationships and first meetings; character knowledge and its acquisition; memories and reported events (source, reliability, witnesses, separate occurrence and recollection dates); commitments and deadlines (offered, accepted, due, performed, canceled); narrative setups, unresolved questions and payoffs. Background characters and ordinary details count. Track unknown explicitly; no invented exact dates, relatives, motives, or identities. Distinguish canonical observations, character reports, assumptions and unresolved proposals. Record both when a fact happened and when the reader learned it; publication order is not story order.

## Every chapter workflow
1. Retrieve latest approved records, relevant provisional records and chapter-specific sources. Build the scene's chronology, character state, location, schedules, knowledge and outstanding obligations. Check compatibility with earlier and later constraints.
2. During drafting, register every new durable detail, transient state, memory, relationship development and commitment in a provisional delta tied to an exact source locator. Record explicitly who learned what and how. Do not assume others know private thoughts or unseen events.
3. After drafting, compare the complete candidate against all relevant records, including cross-character and cross-chapter consequences. Run Python structural checks: unique IDs, valid source references and statuses, date/time order, cycles, knowledge exclusions, balance arithmetic, and no premature promotion. Run semantic continuity review for contradictions not detectable by schema. Validate character voice and history, recurrence of minor characters, physical continuity, travel feasibility, injuries, possession changes, time passage and unfinished promises. Use exact evidence rather than a generic score.
4. Resolve material contradictions in prose or document an explicit author-approved retcon; never silently replace canon. Mark ambiguity as unknown. Schedule reminders as narrative obligations, not real-world notification tasks. Distinguish future plans from completed events.
5. On explicit approval only, lock exact version/hash, merge the delta transactionally into approved history, update current state and retained historical values, run validation and NDM 2.0 distillation, and save a new verified private archive/sidecar. Supplement registration remains subject to the existing supplement-aware archive gate. If promotion cannot be validated, keep records provisional and block advancement.
6. Before writing the next chapter, query this ledger as required input. Every unresolved fact must remain unresolved until established by evidence or explicit author decision.

## Acceptance
A structural test pass proves only the tested invariants; it does not certify semantic continuity, reader response, or authorship. Require zero unresolved material contradictions, verified source identity, correct approval status and an explicit list of open intentional unknowns. All story details and unpublished manuscript material remain private. Public GitHub contains only this procedure and nonsensitive aggregate status.
