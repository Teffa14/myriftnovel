# Serial quality protocol v1.0 — The Last Draw

Mandatory alongside the existing editorial, continuity, originality, and competitive gates. Do not replace evidence with a score, change approved prose, impose a fixed cliffhanger quota, or advance with unresolved blockers.

## Chapter and rolling-arc review

Evaluate each candidate from reader, editor, and author perspectives. Read the relevant previous chapters, current arc and unresolved promises; compare this chapter's contribution with the accumulated reader experience. Identify specific textual evidence and causal consequences, not generic claims about quality. For every criterion record supported / weak / not applicable, an evidence locator, and any repair. Scores, if used, are explicit editorial judgments; no fabricated reader data.

| Criterion | Evidence question |
|---|---|
| Narrative tension | What could be lost, missed, or changed now? |
| Escalation | What becomes harder or more consequential? |
| Anticipation | Which concrete future event has earned interest? |
| Payoff | Which previous expectation receives a meaningful result? |
| Progression | What new ability, access, knowledge, reputation, relationship, or choice is observable? |
| Power fantasy | What credible skill or strategic success produces satisfaction, including useful discoveries within losses? |
| Character appeal | What distinctive action, preference, competence or personality makes this character worth following? |
| Fanservice | What audience interest is rewarded naturally, especially authentic competitive strategy and community texture? |
| Sensual appeal | Where appropriate, is adult attractiveness integrated into action, appearance or chemistry without compulsory sexualization? |
| Chemistry | What interpersonal exchange creates interest or changes the relationship? |
| Memorability | What specific action, line or consequence may be remembered, and why? |
| Emotional range | How does the chapter's register vary across the recent arc? |
| Arc contribution | Which local objective, obstacle, payoff or consequence advances the larger arc? |
| Reader curiosity | What consequential question remains genuinely open? |
| Community appeal | Which genuine choice, character preference or competitive disagreement could readers discuss? |
| Commercial promise | Does the delivered experience align with the work's competitive sports-fiction identity? |

The criteria are diagnostic, not a requirement to force every device into every chapter. Adult sensual appeal must remain context-appropriate; never substitute promotional descriptions, automatic attraction or romance for character agency. Competitive fanservice requires actual scoped evidence and independent opponent choices. Results, spectacle, recognition and useful discoveries must be earned, not asserted.

## Private pilot and payoff ledger

Maintain an author-reviewed private 20-chapter map with chapter number, POV, starting state, objective, opposition, reader-facing consequence, setup/payoff links, forward question, required evidence scope, and dates/commitments. Preserve approved units. Review rolling groups of four chapters for meaningful delivered rewards and escalation, treating the window as a diagnostic rather than a quota. If preparation, negotiation or analysis accumulates without delivery, revise future briefs to bring a consequential action or visible demonstration forward, while preserving causal prerequisites and later milestones. Quiet chapters may provide relief, attachment or consequences; do not make them all tournaments. No invented actual event schedules, fictional result predetermined regardless of legal play, or unapproved character deck assignment.

Before drafting, identify the expected payoff and its evidence. After drafting, locate what the manuscript actually delivers; update the private plan only after author acceptance. Escalate unresolved issues to editorial revision, not automatic approval. Separate subjective commercial assessments from actual consented feedback and observed retention. Reader-facing feedback, if available, records cohort, question, count, date and dissent; never infer commercial validation from internal reviews.

## Acceptance and boundaries

Existing G1–G6 remain mandatory. Commercial strength cannot compensate for unsupported rules, continuity contradictions, material originality reuse, or missing approved archive. Quality review cannot establish publication rights or guarantee income or retention. Keep chapter drafts, precise future beat maps, spoilers, private records and reader data out of public Git. Link private evidence and report only public-safe workflow status.

## Ordinary-life pressure (AD-0003)
Use 05_PLOT_ARCHITECTURE/LIFE_PRESSURE.md. Review whether the chapter or rolling three-chapter window shows a non-game constraint experienced directly, a consequential choice and persistent effect. Distinguish demonstrated pressure from reported scheduling, exposition and melodrama. Record evidence locator, ledger/schedule dependency, independent character goal, consequence, and justified N/A or repair. Across each arc verify a non-game subplot with actual setup and payoff. No fictional hardship automatically increases quality or reader retention.

## Ensemble life and character development (AD-0007)
Apply 04_CHARACTERS/DEVELOPMENT_REQUIREMENTS.md. Require evidence of actual character-specific development rather than exposition about interests, work or money. Maintain private CD status/locators and consequence records; audit all six principal characters and ten diagnostic dimensions at each arc close. Missing evidence requires a concrete revision or justified deferral with a next review point. Preserve historical scores and private mandates. No invented reader data, compulsory per-chapter conflict/romance quota or automatic score increase.

## Slice of life (AD-0008)
Apply 05_PLOT_ARCHITECTURE/SLICE_OF_LIFE.md. Check whether everyday pleasure, TCG lifestyle beyond training, independent social life and comfort are actually experienced on page. Record SL IDs, textual locators and narrative contributions. Review four-main-chapter windows and every arc; explain gaps and identify suitable next opportunities. A quiet scene may earn its place through humor, familiarity, rest or connection without escalating stakes. Preserve technical, continuity, originality and approval gates.

## Long-range emotional architecture (AD-0010)
Apply 05_PLOT_ARCHITECTURE/LONG_RANGE_PAYOFFS.md. Check actual source-linked setup, varied reinforcement, independent causal threads, meaningful recontextualization and aftermath. Judge whether the payoff works without the callback; remove explanatory repetition or unearned claims. Review dormant/open seeds and shorter rewards across arcs. No mandatory catastrophe, fixed emotional-response score or fabricated audience evidence.

## AD-0010-E01 dense-scene review
For a selected dossier, evaluate actual attachment, causal inevitability in hindsight, individual agency, lasting consequences and independent readability. Record supported/weak/deferred with source evidence rather than predicted reader emotions. Compare recent scenes for repeated causes of distress, objects, gestures and revelations. Preserve rest, humor and earned rewards; no mandatory quota of catastrophes or emotional-response guarantees. Respect all private directive and continuity constraints.

## AD-0010-E02 — Major-payoff acceptance
Reject a major scene plan built solely on a lost appointment, financial setback, generic breakup or sentimental object without the earlier lived future, causal turning point and consequential aftermath. Audit independent choices, irreversibility when relevant, distinct mechanisms and actual prior evidence. DS05–40 are rejected historical candidates, not active requirements. No new proposed scene is approved by this correction.

## AD-0011 / AD-0010-E03 — Endpoint and payoff audit
The fixed main-story endpoint is Chapter 500, with supplements excluded. At each arc close audit actual setup, sustained attachment, independent choices, causal turning points and aftermath against the private EM candidate records, not an arbitrary suffering score. At Chapters 120, 240, 360, 440, 460 and 480 review closure capacity and earned progression. Chapter 500 requires resolution of the central dramatic and competitive promises, credible aftermath and a distinct continuation boundary. No delayed primary resolution in Chapter 501 or retroactive cancellation of prior consequences. Additional specific candidate scenes require author acceptance.

## AD-0012 — Reader attachment and enduring absence
Major-payoff review must examine five independently evidenced elements: (1) an identifiable desired future, (2) lived setup and attachment, (3) a credible causal turning point, (4) what remains possible after the event, and (5) what cannot return. Require real consequences, distinct mechanisms, character agency and earned closure. Mark unsupported or premature payoffs for revision or deferral. This is a literary acceptance gate, not a numerical prediction of distress. The author seeks profound, lasting reader emotion; actual voluntary feedback may help evaluate the objective but no reviewer may fabricate testimonials, solicit a scripted reaction or certify impact without audience evidence. Do not reopen approved prose.

## AD-0013 — Original voice review
Use 02_STYLE_BIBLE/TLD_VOICE_METHOD.md and descriptive voice_audit.py. Compare actual openings, closing modes, focal attention, dialogue movement, paragraph rhythm and recurring constructions in comparable scenes. Require textual evidence and contextual reasons for edits or retention, then preserve exact continuity and technical claims. A numerical count does not establish authorship or improvement. Distinguish author-reported external detector output, local descriptive measurements, qualitative editorial review and genuine reader feedback. No fabricated independent review or premature certification.

## AD-0014 — CH20 special finale review
Assess CH20 at arc scale as well as chapter scale. Check source-linked fulfillment of the A01 reader promise, earned cumulative reward, independent character agency, unresolved threads and justified deferrals, visible consequences and aftermath, and a concrete new situation for A02. Evaluate the distinction from an ordinary installment through structure and payoff, not word count alone. Do not require a championship, tragedy, fixed number of callbacks or universal cast attendance; preserve all competitive and continuity evidence. Record actual editorial findings and limitations, not predicted guaranteed reader reaction. Normal author approval and G6 remain mandatory.

AD-0014-A01 editorial gate: review CH15–19 as one causal progression toward CH20 Grand Finale d'Arc. Require each candidate's local reward and outgoing consequence to be evidenced on page, and each subsequent candidate's incoming dependency to be traceable to approved evidence. Inspect for interchangeable episodes, missing transitions, forced cliffhangers, knowledge leaks, repetition and premature resolution. Reconcile the sequence against actual competitive results, character agency and timing rather than prescribing outcomes. At CH20 assess the whole chain and the earlier pilot together.
