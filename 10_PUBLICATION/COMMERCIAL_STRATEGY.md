# Commercial strategy v1.0
Primary creative reference: real Riftbound. Publishing route: pursue rights review for commercially distributing a story using real game references and material; do not assume fan-content or digital-tool policy permits a paid novel. Riot's Legal Jibber Jabber generally limits derivative fan projects to noncommercial use; paid access requires written agreement unless a specific exception applies. Source https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal ; tool policy https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/riftbound is separate. Not legal advice. Do not use logos/art on covers, product or marketing; no endorsement implied. 
Fallback fixed if commercial clearance is denied/unavailable: independently design original competitive TCG with different expression, card names, rules implementation and assets; retain human story, independently validate mechanics and deck engine. No public digital clone and no simply renaming cards. This is a contingency, not replacing the user's current Riftbound premise in internal writing. 
Positioning: English serial focused on competitive drama with high accessibility, identifiable player struggle, real choices and earned growth. 20-chapter pilot, 3 launch chapters then 3/week with reserve, only after clearance. Prioritize retention over paid acquisition; title/subtitle and cover test before launch. Distribution options Royal Road/WebNovel or independent serial subject to current terms, AI disclosure, rights, exclusivity; later ebook/audio after evidence. Do not open accounts or sign anything automatically.
Model after rights: free entry → optional paid early access → ebook volumes, then audio. At 10,000 active readers, illustrative 2% at $10 = $2,000 gross/month (not forecast), expenses and conversion unknown. Goal 10,000 active unique in 30-day period; checkpoints 100, 1,000, 10,000. Acquisition through consent-based relevant communities, no spam; share genuine original discussion and tactical content without copying Riot assets.
 
No public manuscript or definitive spoilers in public Git. No monetization without legal clearance. Do not promise sales. Market validation thresholds in MARKET_VALIDATION.md; revisit before launch.

WebNovel is the current author-selected platform identity as reported by the author. Exact title and supplied cover recorded in BRAND_IDENTITY.md. Do not overwrite cover/listing or infer publication/monetization status. Rights clearance remains unresolved; existing image is not evidence of authorization.
